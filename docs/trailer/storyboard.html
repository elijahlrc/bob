Battle of Builds: the 30-second trailer

One idea, told in three beats that match the game's loop: you build it, you watch it fight, then it fights other players' builds. Every shot is real gameplay captured from the game; the only made-for-trailer parts are the text cards and the edit.

  • 30.0 s · 15 shots
  • 16:9 1920×1080, 60 fps (9:16 cut-down noted per shot)
  • Music 120 bpm, 15 bars of 2 s
  • Text on cards and overlays, no voice-over

Acts on top, shots in the middle (tap one to jump to it), bars and beats at the bottom. Cuts land on beats; the big hits land on bar lines.

Capture notes

  • Record in the browser at 1920×1080 with the Interface size at its default; hide the cursor except in the build shots.
  • Showcase on the title screen auto-plays a run (N picks the next class); the Skill gallery shows one skill against three dummies at down to 0.25× speed, which is where the slow-motion comes from.
  • The Debug panel (dev build or ?debug) forces a biome or a density style, so each montage cut can be set up on purpose instead of waited for.
  • Build a late character (map 40 or later) for the action shots: more skills on the bar, bigger numbers, more on screen.
  • Every name on screen is the game's own. Before export, check that no frame, menu or overlay shows anything from the reference game.
