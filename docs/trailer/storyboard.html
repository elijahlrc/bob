Battle of Builds: the 30-second trailer

For players who love ARPGs and roguelikes. The pitch: every run is a new build, crafted from the tree, linked gems and rolled gear, and taken from level 1 to 100 in a single sitting. Then the build you made duels other players' builds for a place on the high scores. Every shot is real gameplay; only the text and the edit are made for the trailer.

  • 30.0 s · 12 shots
  • 16:9 1920×1080, 60 fps
  • Music 120 bpm, 15 bars of 2 s
  • Text on cards and overlays, no voice-over

Acts on top, shots in the middle (tap one to jump to it), bars and beats at the bottom. Cuts land on beats; the big hits land on bar lines.

Every line of text, in order

    Capture notes

    • Record in the browser at 1920×1080 with the Interface size at its default; hide the cursor except in the crafting inserts. Shot 6 is the exception: three portrait recordings from a real phone, so the trailer shows the game plays there too.
    • Prepare six late saves, one per class, each with a different main skill and damage colour: the Vanguard and Shade for the two runs, the Strider, Reaver and Zealot for the phone shot, the Mystic for the climb. Any one of them can be the duellist.
    • Showcase on the title screen auto-plays a run; the Skill gallery plays one skill against three dummies at down to 0.25× speed for clean slow motion. The Debug panel (dev build or ?debug) forces a biome so each cut is set up on purpose.
    • The high scores and rival names are staged: duel snapshots named oostin, Sin and Eli (real players, names used with their say-so) and the made-up Gravewick, marrowbyte, Tessaly and Ninefingers. Shoot on a local or test database so the live board is untouched.
    • Every name on screen is the game's own. Before export, check that no frame shows anything from the reference game.
