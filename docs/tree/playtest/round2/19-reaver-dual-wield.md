# Playtest round 2, build 19: Reaver dual-wield swordsman

Tester brief: Reaver (str/dex), two swords, attack speed, accuracy, crits, block or evasion. Budget 100 points; 98 spent. About 22 tool calls used.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a six-spoke wheel with a very small hub. The regions are huge, loose fields of small clusters joined by thin grey roads. Notables are scattered widely, so at first glance I could not tell where anything is.
- The labelled landmarks (Shieldwall, Lone Vow, Plated Hide, Wound Dance) are all at the far rim. The big discs shown on the whole picture are mostly keystones I did not want. The overview picture tells me where keystones are but nothing about where weapon or crit nodes are.
- The Reaver region picture is much better. It is dense with notables carrying readable names. Weapon-family names cluster in recognisable groups: Paired Fury, Left-Hand Gambit, Mirrored Duelist and Twinsteel Pact are obviously the dual-wield cluster. Cudgel Cadence and Drover's Cudgel are maces. Headsman's Weight and Both-Hands Oath are two-handed. Hewing Rhythm and Whetted Hatchet are axes. Mailsplitter Cut and Riposte Edge are swords. I could plan from the picture alone, which was a real pleasure.
- What looks pointless: the region has a lot of notables for weapons I will never hold (axes, maces, 2H, bows, totems, auras), and a big block of Mana, Intelligence-only and "Max Fervour/Grit Charge" notables. The picture is mostly filler for any given build. The numeric suffixes on the labels (1189 etc.) are noise to a player.
- The hub and the spokes look like a thin web around the start nodes. I could not tell from the picture whether the middle gives anything.

## 2. Allocation log

1. Ran `region strdex`. The list is nearest first with costs. It is very useful: I could see Paired Fury at 6, Sidearm Tithe at 5, Mailsplitter at 7, Riposte Edge at 8, Left-Hand Gambit at 14, Mirrored Duelist at 16, Twinsteel Pact at 17, Spurred Haste at 14. Paired Fury was right next to the start and is huge (+50% attack damage, +20% attack speed, +50% DoT while dual wielding). I took it first. The walk went Damage, Strength, Max Life, then two dual-wield block smalls (+3% block and +10% damage each), then the notable. Every step on the way was something I wanted. Cost 6.
2. Mailsplitter Cut (4 more points after Paired Fury; the path shared nodes) then Riposte Edge (3 more), then Sidearm Tithe (3 more). The cost of each dropped as my build grew near them, which made it easy to plan. The roads gave Armour, Life and Damage with Swords. Cost 4, 3, 3 (total 16).
3. Left-Hand Gambit, then Mirrored Duelist, then Spurred Haste, then Twinsteel Pact. Walk to Gambit was 10 (life, evasion, dexterity, armour, dual-wield damage smalls). Mirrored Duelist 5 more, Spurred Haste 5 more, Twinsteel Pact 4 more. All on the way was either evasion, armour, life or dual-wield damage. Total after this: 40 points. This part felt great, because my whole sword and dual-wield and block core was in the one south-south-west corridor.
4. Searched for crit and accuracy. `find "Critical Strike Chance"` (39 notables), `find "Critical Strike Multiplier"`, `find "Accuracy"`, `find Sword`, `find "Dual Wield"`. Result: the Reaver region has only Riposte Edge for swords-crit. The only generic accuracy and crit-chance notables near me were Chisel Eye (cost 13) and Pincer Cadence (12 at first, 2 after Chisel Eye) in the neighbouring Vanguard (str) region. I walked west to take Chisel Eye (13 points of road: life, strength, damage, regen, armour, accuracy) and then Pincer Cadence (2). Total 55.
5. Mailed Dancer (4) and Brigandine Stride (3) for the evasion and armour. They are both back in the south of Reaver, so after going west to the Vanguard I came back. Total 62.
6. Keen Chisel (16 points, from Chisel Eye, a long road through Vanguard life, strength, armour smalls) and Splitstone Insight (7 more). Melee crit chance +180% total and +75% crit multiplier. That is the crit payoff. Total 85. This was the longest walk of the build and nearly all of it was Strength, Life, Armour, Damage. These smalls were not what I wanted but were not useless either.
7. Quickforge Hand, Rapid Anvil (12% attack speed), Stonehewn Brawn, Tombhearth Blood, Monolith Skin, all within a couple of points of what I already had in Vanguard. Total 98. Two points left, deliberately unspent (nothing good was cheaper than 3).
8. I considered Shieldwall (the Reaver block keystone: +10% max block, -30% evasion) and decided against it because I took Mailed Dancer and Brigandine Stride for evasion, and the keystone cancels some of that. I took no keystone; no keystone fit a dual-wield sword crit build.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 98 of 100 points. 19 notables, 0 keystones, 66 small nodes, 13 road or attribute nodes. 50 points in strdex, 47 in str (Vanguard), 1 in hub.
- Notables: Paired Fury, Mailsplitter Cut, Riposte Edge, Sidearm Tithe, Left-Hand Gambit, Mirrored Duelist, Spurred Haste, Twinsteel Pact, Chisel Eye, Pincer Cadence, Mailed Dancer, Brigandine Stride, Keen Chisel, Splitstone Insight, Quickforge Hand, Rapid Anvil, Stonehewn Brawn, Tombhearth Blood, Monolith Skin.
- Totals (from `summary`): +24% block while dual wielding; 148% inc. damage with attacks while dual wielding; 41% attack speed (plus 12% while dual wielding and more with swords or melee); 45% accuracy and +120 accuracy; 180% crit chance with melee, 70% generic crit chance, 40% with swords; +75% crit multiplier with melee, +47% with swords; 226% armour, 72% evasion; 93% max life; +14% elemental resistances; +160 Strength, +70 Dexterity.
- Score: 8 out of 10. The core dual-wield, block, attack-speed and swords are all delivered, mostly in my own region, and a crit layer is reachable. Lost points: crit and accuracy exist only through melee-generic nodes in the neighbouring Vanguard region (a long walk away) and the dual-wield accuracy and crit chance are not in my class's home region. About 25 of my 98 points went on small nodes like Strength and Armour that I did not pick for themselves (they are fine value, but not my plan). I also did not get a way to use the middle of the tree.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree; I reached a build I like in about twenty calls. The two biggest problems: crit chance, crit multiplier and accuracy for a sword dual-wielder are almost all outside the home region, so you walk 13 to 16 points west through Strength and Armour filler; and the overview picture says nothing about where weapon or crit nodes are. The two best things: the dual-wield spine in Reaver with on-theme smalls on the road, and costs that fall as the build grows. The middle of the tree gave me nothing I wanted.
