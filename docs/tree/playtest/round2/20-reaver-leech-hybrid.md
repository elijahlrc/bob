# Playtest round 2, build 20: Reaver, leech and hybrid defence fighter

Brief: one-handed weapon, life leech and life on hit, armour and evasion together, resistances, decent damage. About 100 points.
Build file: `scratchpad/audit2/20.json`. I used 98 of 100 points (I stopped two short, see 3).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

I looked at the whole-tree picture and the Reaver (strdex) region picture before using the tool.

- The whole tree is a big wheel. Six class starts sit around a small middle and long spokes run out to six dense regions. Each region is a dense mesh with sparse fringes, and the keystones sit on the outer edge as lonely big discs. The big picture reads well as a map: I could see at once that the Reaver's region is the bottom, that Shieldwall, Plated Hide and Lone Vow are the three keystones at the bottom edge, and that Wound Dance and Nimble Gambit are on the right.
- The Reaver region picture is readable once zoomed, but it is a lot of orange dots with names that all look alike ("... Cadence", "... Hatchet", "... Cudgel"). The weapon families are grouped in space: axes and two-handers upper left, maces upper middle, swords and shield and dual-wield lower left, bleed and totems right, mana and charges right. That grouping is the best thing about the picture, but I could not know it without reading all the names. The picture does not say which weapon a notable is for unless I read the name and know the word.
- Leech notables ("Sidearm Tithe", "Sandcup Reprieve", "Arena Thirst") are near the Reaver start, which I did not expect from the picture alone, because they are not labelled with anything leech-like. I found them only through `find`.
- What looks pointless: the long grey road spokes between the middle and the outer regions (they look like empty highway); the mana-only notables (Quartermaster's Reserve, Wayfarer's Flask, Strategist's Tent) sitting right next to the start of a strength-dexterity class; the "1 to Maximum Grit/Fervour Charges" notables that look important but give nothing on their own.
- Way to find my way: by region colour and the labelled keystones, then `find` for the rest. The pictures alone were not enough to plan a leech build, `find leech` was.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Plan.** I ran `legend`, then `reach 8` from the empty build. I saw one leech notable at cost 5 (Sidearm Tithe, which is also the one-handed weapon notable) and Sandcup Reprieve and Arena Thirst at 7 and 9. That is a good start: the first reach screen already had what the brief asked for.
2. **Searches.** `find leech`, `find leeched`: 16 notables and keystones, with the three closest all in my region and the rest 17+ away in other regions (Crimson Pact keystone in the hub, Thirsting Bellows in str, Fevered Nick in dexint). `find "life per"`, `find "life on"`, `find "life gained"`, `find "when you hit"`, `find "per enemy hit"`, `find hit`: no node gives life on hit. The nearest are "Gain 5/10 Life per Enemy Killed" (Cutthroat's Spoils, Spoils of the Alley, both in dexint, cost 28+). So I could not get the "life on hit" half of the brief.
3. **Sidearm Tithe (#1442), 5 points.** Road: Damage, Strength, two one-handed weapon smalls, then the notable. Every node on the way was something I wanted (7% damage, one-handed damage and attack speed).
4. **Paired Fury (#1189), 4 points.** Path went through Maximum Life and two "Block while Dual Wielding" nodes. I wanted it for the unconditional 50% increased attack damage and 20% attack speed. The dual-wield-only block nodes on the way are dead for a one-hander (+6% block, no use).
5. **Arena Thirst (#1281), 7 points, and Sandcup Reprieve (#1278), 2 points.** Path to Arena Thirst gave evasion, damage, strength and two leech-speed nodes. I took the leech trio: 0.6 + 1.0 + 0.8 = 2.4% leech at 18 points. Good.
6. **Armour and evasion.** `find armour`, `find evasion`: the only notables that give both are Mailed Dancer, Brigandine Stride and Gambeson Mantle (all strdex, y 3000 to 3700, a long way south) and Shared Sorrow in the hub (cost 19). `find "armour and evasion"` shows only four small nodes, all in the hub. I walked to Mailed Dancer (**13 points**, passing through a lot of armour/life/evasion smalls and one more Damage), then Brigandine Stride (5) and Gambeson Mantle (4). Total 22 points for three notables, with about 15 small nodes on the way that were each fine (life, armour, evasion, strength, dexterity). The walk gave something, but it was 13 points before the first notable.
7. **Shield nodes.** A one-hander and a shield is the natural hybrid. Rampart Windfall (3), Bulwark Vow (4), Shieldwall Drill (2), Rivetbreaker (2, armour and physical reduction penetration), Tower Shield Rank (4). Cheap once I was in the south because they all sit in the same area as the armour notables.
8. **Damage and speed.** Spurred Haste (5, +12% attack speed), Mailsplitter Cut (3, sword), Spoils Draught (2, more leech speed), Lancepoint Charge (4, physical damage and attack speed).
9. **Resistances.** Mailed Dancer +10 and Gambeson +12 gave 22%. I wanted more, so I took Wanderer's Tithe (hub, 6 points). The road went through a Maximum Energy Shield node and Evasion Rating, so the notable cost 6 for +8% resists and 5% life, about 20 resistance points in total across the hub nodes (42% total).
10. **Red Gait (#1450, 6 points):** 10% more damage while leeching. A good fit, found by `reach` after the leech base was in.
11. **Crimson Pact (#58, 12 points).** I wanted instant leech. The 12-point road through the hub was mostly mana, energy shield and damage nodes (which a Reaver does not want), so the keystone was effectively paid for with about 5 dead nodes. A real discovery: the keystone says "no Life Regeneration".
12. **Mistake.** After taking Crimson Pact, I took Veteran's Sinew (5 points) for 8% physical melee and +20 Strength, and only noticed afterwards that its "Regenerate 1% of Life per second" line and the 0.4% regen smalls on the road are cancelled by Crimson Pact. The `summary` still lists "Regenerate 1.4% of Life per second" as a total next to "you have no Life Regeneration", so nothing warns about that. About 3 points wasted.
13. I stopped at 98 points because nothing in `reach` was worth the two points left.

## 3. The build I ended with

- Points: 98 of 100. 19 notables, 1 keystone, 68 small, 10 roads/attributes. 82 in the strdex region, 16 in the hub.
- Notables: Sidearm Tithe, Paired Fury, Arena Thirst, Sandcup Reprieve, Spoils Draught, Mailed Dancer, Brigandine Stride, Gambeson Mantle, Rivetbreaker, Rampart Windfall, Bulwark Vow, Shieldwall Drill, Tower Shield Rank, Spurred Haste, Mailsplitter Cut, Lancepoint Charge, Red Gait, Wanderer's Tithe, Veteran's Sinew. Keystone: Crimson Pact.
- Totals (from `summary`): 1.9% damage leeched as life (1.6% of it physical-only) plus 0.8% as mana, 140% increased leech rate, instant leech; 73% increased maximum life; 188% increased armour and 156% increased evasion; +42% all elemental resistances; 225% increased defences from shield, +11% block with a shield; 50% increased attack damage, 23% increased attack speed plus 20% while holding a shield; 77% increased damage plus a lot of conditional damage.
- Score **8/10**. Leech, one-hander, armour plus evasion, resistances and damage all came out as asked, and the leech notables were close to the start. I lose points for: no life on hit anywhere (half of the brief), only 42% resist split over many small nodes (no 75% cap in sight, and I do not know what cap the game uses), the conflicting regen line (my own mistake, but the tool did not warn), and the big conditional damage text (a shield, a sword, a dual wield) that means several of my damage lines may not apply at the same time. I cannot tell what the "while Dual Wielding" lines give me.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree: the cost column and `find` made a plan in about 20 calls. The two biggest problems: no life on hit anywhere, and roads to the hub (and to the southern defences) that are padded with mana and energy shield or weapon-conditioned nodes I cannot use. The two best things: the leech cluster right next to a Reaver start, and the way every road to the south gave life, armour and evasion nodes that I actually wanted. The middle of the tree gave me one keystone and one resistance notable, not worth the detour except for Crimson Pact.
