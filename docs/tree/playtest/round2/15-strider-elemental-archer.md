# Playtest round 2, build 15: Strider, elemental archer (cold + lightning bow)

Build file: scratchpad/audit2/15.json. 99 of 100 points spent (I stopped one short because nothing at cost 1 was worth taking).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole-tree picture reads as six petals around a small hub, with a lot of dark empty space between and around them. The stars are tiny and the labels are only legible for keystones and a few notables.
- I can find my way by region. The coloured keystone discs (Steady Draw, Close Quarters, Arrow Weave, Nimble Gambit, Wound Dance) sit out in the lower-right petal, so Strider is clearly "the bow and evasion corner". That is good orientation.
- The Strider region picture is dense. I could not pick notables out by eye. The names are fun and say what they are (Rimebite Barb for cold, Stormfeather Barb for elemental), so I could guess before opening the tool. Only the tool gave me real numbers.
- Lightning is not in my own region. The region picture shows it as one notable (Sparkwick Trance, at the top left, near the Shade start). Real lightning-for-attacks lives in the Shade (dexint) petal, to the north. That is plausible for a Strider-Shade split, but the picture does not tell you that.
- What looks pointless at first glance: the big grid of repeated clusters in the lower-left and in the upper-left of the dexint picture (trap and mine notables, cost 14-18 each). They are dense and irrelevant to me. The middle hub looked like a pretty ring and nothing more, until the tool showed it has notables (see section 3).

## 2. Allocation log

1. Wanted: elemental damage, penetration and freeze for an archer right at the start. `reach 8` from the Strider start listed Prismfeather Shot (cost 6), Stormfeather Barb and Rimebite Barb (7 each) and Frostfletch Gale (8). This was a very good first screen. I took all four (15 points). The walk was not filler: Attack Speed, Dexterity, Movement Speed, Elemental Damage with Attacks, Evasion, Cold Damage. I now had 70% elemental attack damage, 11% elemental penetration, 8% cold penetration and +10% freeze.
2. Wanted: bow damage and physical-to-cold conversion. I took Hoarthorn Frost (2 points, 20% physical converted to cold), then Whistling Shaft (3), Ridge Archer's Pace (4, bow damage plus 120 evasion plus movement speed) and Shaftpiercer Hunt (3). That brought me to 27. All of these were within 2-4 points of the previous one, and the roads between them were mostly Bow Damage, Evasion and Attack Speed smalls. I could chain without detours.
3. Wanted: a big multiplier. I took the keystone Steady Draw (6 points: 35% more bow damage, 20% less bow attack speed). The road to it was four "Bow Damage" smalls and a Maximum Life, so I did not feel the walk. I was at 33.
4. Wanted: lightning. `find lightning` showed that all attack-relevant lightning is in the dexint petal (Sparkwick Trance, Forked Glint, Jolted Mark). The rest of the 13 hits are int or strint nodes at cost 33+ (spell-flavoured, plus Searing Avatar, which I definitely do not want). I took Sparkwick Trance first (8 points, 16% lightning damage and 10% shock effect; 41 points).
5. Then Jolted Mark (16 points, 57 total) and Forked Glint (4 more, 61 total). The walk to Jolted Mark was long: 15 smalls, including four Maximum Energy Shield and two Intelligence nodes, which do nothing for an evasion archer. It gave me some Damage and Critical Strike Chance, but around a third of the walk was dead weight. I was committed once I wanted shock chance and 24% lightning damage with attacks.
6. Wanted: evasion. Arrow Weave (40% projectile evade) cost 12 from my build. The road was Evasion, Movement Speed, Attack Speed and Dexterity nodes, so it was a good walk. I took it (73). Then Mistcloak Drift (2), Gale-Footed Stalk (2) and Lichen Veil (3, with +8% elemental resistances, useful against elemental enemies). That made 80.
7. Spent the last 19 points on Prism Shutter (3, 16% elemental damage), Cellar Frost (4, cold damage and chill), Wildwood Skirmisher (2), Eagle's Windmark (4, big evasion and accuracy), Lone Hawk's Cry (3, 80% bow crit) and Wide Shadow Cast (3). Total 99.
8. Not taken, considered: Hawk's Zenith (crit multiplier for bows, cost 4), Close Quarters (keystone, cost 6, the sort of gamble I would not take blind), Tailwind Shaft, the Hub notables (Compass of Marrow, 16% damage and 4% attack speed, only cost 3 from my build at the end). I ran out of points, which is a good sign: there were more useful choices than points.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 99/100 points. 20 notables, 2 keystones (Steady Draw, Arrow Weave), 65 smalls, 12 roads and attributes. Regions: dex 63, dexint 35, hub 1.
- Totals (from `summary`, abridged): 304% increased Evasion Rating, +210 flat evasion, 40% Evade Projectile Attacks; 144% increased Damage with Bows and 35% more bow damage; 70% increased Elemental Damage with Attacks plus 36% Elemental Damage; 66% increased Cold Damage and 58% with Attacks; 64% increased Lightning Damage with Attacks; elemental penetration 11%, cold 8%, lightning 8%; 20% of physical converted to cold; +10% Freeze, +25% Shock chance, 35% Shock effect, 50% Chill effect; 80% bow crit chance bonus; 24% attack speed (minus 20% from Steady Draw); +12% all elemental resistances; 15% more maximum Life (low).
- Match with the brief: 8/10. Everything I asked for was there: cold, lightning, penetration, freeze, shock, evasion. The weak spots are:
  - Freeze is only +10% (two sources, the other is 30+ points away in int).
  - Shock chance is modest at +25%.
  - Life is thin (15% increased, +12), so survival is evasion only (that is the brief, though).
  - Penetration is split across three separate lines (general elemental 11%, cold 8%, lightning 8%) and I could not tell from the sheet how they add.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree. The bow and evasion corner is coherent, and `reach` with costs makes it easy to reason about what to do next. The two biggest problems: lightning and freeze are far from a Strider (lightning is 15-16 points into the Shade petal, freeze barely exists), and some roads into the other petals carry nothing that helps (Energy Shield, Intelligence). The two best things: the first 15 points deliver a full elemental archer; keystone walks are made of useful smalls (Steady Draw, Arrow Weave). The hub gave me little.
