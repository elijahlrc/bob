# Playtest 13: Vanguard, fire melee bruiser (round 2)

Build file: scratchpad/audit2/13.json. Brief: fire damage and ignite with melee attacks, lots of life, fire resistance, recovery. 99 of 100 points spent.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole-tree picture reads as a hub with six wedges and long thin spokes. The six class starts sit on an inner ring, close to each other. Each wedge is a field of small clusters, with a few honeycomb-looking "city" areas (top middle, bottom middle, lower left) and some long ruled lines with clusters hanging off them (top left, right).
- Keystones and a few named notables are labelled far out on the rim (Searing Avatar and Living Ward far left, Fever Pitch top, Shade Leech and Hollow Vessel right, Plated Hide and Lone Vow bottom). Looking at the picture, I guessed the keystones sit at the ends of long lines. That held up.
- Where I would go: my class is lower left (Vanguard Start). The region picture for str is dense, with the labels crowded into one corner (Blood Rite, Rooted Stance, Idle Hands, Unerring Discipline on the far edge). "Fire" does not appear as a word in the picture. The red/orange dots read as "life" and "fire" at once, so I could not pick the fire clusters from colour. The tool's `find fire` was what located them.
- Looked pointless: the area directly around the six class starts looks like a wheel with nothing in it. The inner ring (hub) nodes look like filler. The honeycomb "city" areas have notables in every cell, so nothing in them stands out. The two fire-labelled areas that I could spot (west of Vanguard at about y 1100, and the upper-left Zealot wedge at about y -1000) are on opposite sides of a long vertical gap.
- I could not tell from the pictures how far anything is in points. The first `reach` made it clear: nothing fire was within 8 points.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Wanted**: find where fire is. `reach 8` from the start returned only one notable (Stake and Mallet, impale). That told me nothing about fire. `find fire` returned 49 nodes: 3 in my own region (Forgeproof Blood, Hearthfire Vigil, Slagheart Kindling), a pocket in the Zealot wedge (Brimstone Hymn, Pyre Bloom, Kindling Penance, Searing Avatar, Smoldering Heresy), the Reaver wedge at the bottom (Torchbrand Wrath, Kindled Steel, "Fire Damage with Attacks" smalls) and the Mystic top (a lot of spell-fire).
2. **Ignite search**: `find ignite` (4 hits), `find burn` (0 hits), `find "Fire Resistance"` (3 hits). Ignite exists only as 4 notables, each at 22 to 34 points from the start: Kindling Penance (Zealot wedge), Ashen Triptych (Zealot wedge), Kindled Steel (Reaver, with a speed-of-ignite line, attack-flavoured) and Smoldering Colophon (Mystic).
3. **Life core: Bloodwell then Forgeproof Blood** (15 points total). Bloodwell cost 9 (the walk was Damage, Life Regen, Strength, Damage, Life Regen, Strength, Strength, Life, then the notable); Forgeproof Blood was 6 more. The walk was useful. Nearly every step gave life, regen or strength. I got a notable with fire res and +1% max fire res for just 15 points. Good.
4. **Marrow Reservoir, Stonehewn Brawn, Mossgrave Rest** (11 points, to 26). All cost 3 to 4 because they branch off the road I already owned. These gave life, melee damage, armour, 1.8% regen. This is what I expected a bruiser to feel like.
5. **Fire pocket in my own region: Slagheart Kindling and Hearthfire Vigil** (16 points, to 42). Slagheart cost 13 and passed through Damage, Life Regen, Strength, then three "Fire Damage" smalls. Hearthfire cost 3 more. The smalls are "10% fire damage and 0.4% regen", so none of it was wasted. Both notables are fire and regen. This pocket works well for the brief.
6. **Ignite: Kindling Penance** (21 points, to 63). It is the cheapest ignite chance for an attacker I found (the Reaver one is 28). The path was bad for my build: Damage, Strength, Life, Regen, Intelligence, Life, Mana, Strength, Strength, Energy Shield, Life, Intelligence, Armour, Life, Mana, Strength, Energy Shield, Intelligence, Fire Damage, Fire Damage, notable. About 8 of those 20 smalls are mana, energy shield or intelligence, which are no use to a melee Vanguard. See friction #3.
7. **Fire pocket in the Zealot wedge, once I was there** (10 points, to 73): Brimstone Hymn (2, extra fire as phys, +phys), Pyre Bloom (3, 25% fire damage), Smoldering Heresy (3, ele pen and DoT mult), Marrow Hymn (2, life). All cheap since I was already standing next to them. This part felt great: after the 21-point walk, the next four notables cost 10 total.
8. **Flagellant's Spark and Ashen Triptych** (8 points, to 81). Flagellant's Spark is melee-crit and attack elemental damage with some pen; it is the one notable in the area clearly made for a melee elemental. Triptych gave +12% all resistance and +5% ignite.
9. **Searing Avatar** (5 points, to 86; Fire Damage x4 then the keystone). I took it because it fits "fire" as a theme. I am not sure it is good (friction #5), but it was cheap.
10. **Back to the Vanguard side to finish**: Quickforge Hand (2), Rapid Anvil (3), Tombhearth Blood (3), Roadworn Plate (5) = 13, to 99. Surprise: I was in the Zealot wedge and these notables, back in my own region, still cost only 2 to 5. The cost display worked well; I only had to trust it.

## 3. The build I ended with

99/100 points. 18 notables, 1 keystone, 63 small, 17 road/attribute. By region: str 59, strint 39, hub 1.

Notables: Bloodwell, Forgeproof Blood, Marrow Reservoir, Stonehewn Brawn, Mossgrave Rest, Slagheart Kindling, Hearthfire Vigil, Kindling Penance, Brimstone Hymn, Pyre Bloom, Smoldering Heresy, Marrow Hymn, Flagellant's Spark, Ashen Triptych, Quickforge Hand, Rapid Anvil, Tombhearth Blood, Roadworn Plate. Keystone: Searing Avatar.

Totals from `summary`:

- 134% maximum life, +36 life, +250 strength
- 7.6% life regen per second
- 124% armour
- +40% all elemental resistances, +8% fire resistance, +1% max fire resistance
- 189% increased fire damage, +10% of phys as extra fire, 24% fire damage with attacks
- +20% chance to ignite, 24% DoT multiplier
- 20% increased stun threshold (not needed)
- Attack speed: 15% plus 5.5% melee
- Leftovers: 30% lightning damage, +40 int, +24% mana, 10% ES, +5% freeze, +5% shock, 15% phys-as-fire conversion with spells (these are all dead weight).

Score: 7/10. Life, regen, armour, fire damage and resistance are strong. Ignite is only +20% chance, with no ignite damage or duration, so it is thin. Recovery is regen only; no leech or flask-recovery node was on the way (Thirsting Bellows and Tankard Mail exist but I did not reach them). Fire resistance: the +8% fire plus 40% all is good but cannot reach a cap without more. About 25 of 99 points were unwanted smalls (mana, ES, int and lightning).

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree: costs after a walk make routes readable. The two biggest problems: the pictures do not show where fire is, and the only route to ignite is a 21-point walk full of mana and energy shield smalls, with no ignite damage anywhere close. The two best things: the west road from Vanguard into the fire/life pocket (every step helped), and the way `reach` re-prices everything once you stand in a new area, which made the second pocket cheap. The middle of the tree gave me nothing.
