# Playtest 23: Shade, trap-and-mine specialist (100 points)

Build file: audit2/23.json. Tool calls used: about 20 (plus 2 pictures read).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big wheel with a tiny hub, six wedges (one per class) and a lot of dark space between clusters. It looks hand-made, not like a regular grid, and I like that. Clusters are connected by long thin grey roads.
- Region labels are only the few big discs (keystones) on the whole picture, so from the whole-tree picture alone I could not find a single notable. I needed the dexint region picture, and even that is dense: about 70 labels, many overlapped by neighbours ("Wide Shadow Cast 717" sits on top of a node, "Pinpoint Stiletto" is clipped).
- In the dexint picture I could read the neighbourhood by names at once: a pile of trap and mine names ("Wide-Mouthed Snare", "Wicked Tripwire", "Patient Powderkeg", "Sleeper Cache", "Gaping Pitfall", "Hairpin Trigger", "Warren of Snares") in the strip right of Shade Start, daggers and claws in the top strip, chaos and damage over time (Gloomblight, Rotwhisper) to the east, evasion and energy shield (Fogbound Ward, Gossamer Dusk, Veilstep) further east. The names themselves tell the build, which is a good sign for way-finding.
- Looks pointless: the number suffixes in the labels (e.g. "Warren of Snares 497") are node ids, so they read as clutter to a player who does not care. The thin diagonal wedge lines and the horizontal line across the middle of the picture mean nothing to me. Mana and spell clusters in the far north looked irrelevant to me (not tested).

## 2. Allocation log

1. Wanted: where traps and mines live. `find trap`, `find mine`: 31 and 29 matches, nearly all in dexint, right beside my start. Cheapest trap notables cost 7 from the start, the first mine notable 7, and "Warren of Snares" (+2 traps and +2 mines at once) 9.
2. Took Warren of Snares first (9 points) because it is the biggest all-round node. The road to it ran through two Damage, two Dexterity, one Energy Shield and a trap damage small: only about 3 of the 8 road nodes were dead weight for me. After it, the other notables dropped to cost 2 or 3 each, which is an excellent feeling: the cluster rewards going in.
3. Took, in order: Wide-Mouthed Snare (2), Powder and Wire (2), Hairpin Trigger (3), Gaping Pitfall (3), Bitter Fuse (3), Wicked Tripwire (3), Patient Powderkeg (2), Patient Tripline (3), Sleeper Cache (3). At 33 points I had 8 notables of traps and mines, 4 extra traps/mines, big damage, area, crit chance and multiplier. Almost nothing on the road was wasted: roads here are nodes for traps, crit, mine damage.
4. Wanted: a bit of chaos or elemental. `find Chaos Damage` and `find Elemental Damage`: the only close chaos/elemental notables are the east cluster (Gloomblight, Alleyrunner's Blight, Rotwhisper Cant, Prism Shutter). Prism Shutter cost 10, but the walk gave ES, evasion, crit chance, dexterity and elemental damage smalls, so the walk paid for itself. Then Gloomblight (4), Alleyrunner (3), Rotwhisper (3). 53 points.
5. Wanted: evasion and energy shield. `find Evasion`, `find Energy Shield`. In the dexint east strip only. Masked Precision (5, crit chance +20% and 20% damage), Fogbound Ward (4), Gossamer Dusk (4), Veilstep (3). 69 points. The roads there are almost all evasion and ES smalls, so the defence comes mostly from the walk, not from the notables. Good.
6. Wanted more crit. `find Critical Strike Chance` (128 matches, many weapon-specific: daggers, claws, bows, swords, spells, none for traps beyond Wicked Tripwire). Took the generic ones in the north-east: Hush Before Cut (7), Keyhole Glimpse (5), Whetted Malice (3). 84 points. Total crit chance +295% (all sources; only 70% / 50% is trap/mine specific).
7. Took Taintedsteel Pace (3): extra chaos from physical, plus some speed. 87 points.
8. 13 points left. Options: Fleet Shadow (6) + Sparkwick Trance (5), or Thornsnare Warren (13, a second trap notable in the dex region: +30% trap damage, +20% area, +2 traps). Took Thornsnare Warren. The road went back through the hub and into the Strider region: Crit, Damage, Dex, Crit, Damage, Strength (hub), Attack Speed, Life, Dex, Movement Speed, Attack Speed, Trap damage small, then the notable. So 13 points for a notable and about 5 useful nodes (trap damage + cast speed small, life, move speed); two Attack Speed nodes are dead for a trap build (traps use cast speed). 100 points.
9. Did not look at: other regions' pictures, nor the str/int regions. `find` for deployable, detonate, throw, laying, cooldown found nothing useful.

## 3. The build I ended with

- Points: 100/100. 23 notables, 0 keystones, 63 small, 14 road/attribute. Regions: dexint 92, hub 1, dex 7.
- Notables: Warren of Snares, Wide-Mouthed Snare, Powder and Wire, Hairpin Trigger, Gaping Pitfall, Bitter Fuse, Wicked Tripwire, Patient Powderkeg, Patient Tripline, Sleeper Cache, Prism Shutter, Gloomblight Draught, Alleyrunner's Blight, Rotwhisper Cant, Masked Precision, Fogbound Ward, Gossamer Dusk, Veilstep, Hush Before Cut, Keyhole Glimpse, Whetted Malice, Taintedsteel Pace, Thornsnare Warren.
- Keystones: none. I saw none that I wanted. Shade Leech (ES leech, 50% less recharge), Cruel Agony (30% less hit damage on crit) and Hollow Vessel are all traps against a crit trapper or a ES build.
- Totals (from `summary`): +302% damage with traps, +151% with mines, +4 extra traps and +4 extra mines, +140% trap area, +16% trap cast speed, +90% / +60% crit multiplier (trap / mine), +70% / +50% crit chance (trap / mine), 295% increased crit chance total, 10% penetration of elemental resistance with both, 112% increased chaos damage, 36% elemental, 75% physical, 168% evasion, 77% maximum ES, +28% chaos res, +16% all elemental res, +10% life.
- Score: 8/10 for the brief. Traps and mines are covered wonderfully, with crit and area and count. Evasion plus ES is done (77% ES, 168% evasion). Chaos and elemental is "a bit". Lost points: the chaos/elemental damage does not say it applies to traps or mines (112% chaos damage helps only if the skill deals chaos damage), mines are noticeably thinner than traps (no mine area, no mine cast speed nearby), and I have no keystone that helps this character.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree. The strongest thing is the neighbourhood: my build's core was a short walk away, and the roads gave me crit, ES and evasion on the way, so no point felt dead. The names are good too.

The two biggest problems: mines are thinner than traps (no area, speed or crit nearby that is mine-only), and chaos/elemental damage cards do not say whether they apply to traps or mines. Also a lone trap notable far through the hub on attack-speed roads, and no keystone for this build. I did not meet the middle of the tree much: the hub gave me Strength and attack-speed nodes only on the way to Thornsnare Warren, so for this build the middle was a poor walk, not a destination.
