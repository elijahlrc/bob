## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-armed star. The centre looks dense and busy (rings, spokes, a lot of tiny dots). The outer areas are long arms with notables and keystones hanging off thin lines.
- The Vanguard region (lower left) is huge: a wide field of notables with long stalks to far keystones (Rooted Stance, Idle Hands, Unerring Discipline, Blood Rite). I could not tell from the picture where the "attack with axes" things were. The labels are all weapon-type names (Quarryman's Sweep, Lone Hammer, Gravemaul Sway...), so I guessed weapon notables would be in the lower part of the Vanguard region. That guess was right (axes at around x -2400, y 2100), but they sit about 24 points away.
- The Reaver region (bottom) is labelled with many weapon names too (Hewing Rhythm, Sandthirst Hatchet, Whetted Hatchet, Crimson Furrow, Arena Thirst, Sandcup Reprieve). From the picture alone it looked like the place for an axe bleeder with leech, which is not what I expected for a "strength class" pick. I did not expect to leave the Vanguard region entirely.
- What stands out: the keystone discs at the ends of long dotted roads (Wound Dance, Plated Hide, Shieldwall). What looks pointless: the Vanguard's far west (Rooted Stance, Idle Hands) and the large empty gaps between clusters. The mid-tree hexagon field in the top (int) looked like decoration to a strength player.
- The Vanguard start sits very close to the hub ring. The picture gives me no sense of how far the Reaver is from it; I only learned that with the tool.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Looked for what I want.** `region str` showed axe notables only at cost 24-25 (Quarryman's Sweep, Rustwater Cleave, Timberfall Chop), with lots of life and armour at 9-17. I then ran `find bleed`, `find axe`, `find leech`. Result: the strdex (Reaver) region has an axe cluster at cost 16-17 (Hewing Rhythm, Sandthirst Hatchet, Whetted Hatchet), a leech cluster at 13-17, and a bleed cluster at 17-19 (Crimson Furrow, Wound Dance at 22). It all lined up in one region. So I decided to leave the Vanguard area almost completely.
2. **Paired Fury (cost 14).** It is the nearest big payoff in strdex: 50% increased Damage with Attacks and 20% increased Attack Speed (the DoT part needs dual wielding, which I will not use). The walk passed through the hub: two Elemental Resistances, two Maximum Energy Shield, a Damage, 2 Life and 2 Strength nodes, then two dual wield block nodes I did not want. So about 6 of the 14 points were ES/resist/block filler. I accepted it because Paired Fury is strong.
3. **Axes (Hewing Rhythm 5, Sandthirst Hatchet 3, Whetted Hatchet 4).** The walk to each was through small "Damage with Axes" nodes, so the walk is good: every road node was something I wanted. Total 26 points, nothing wasted.
4. **Leech.** Sidearm Tithe (3; 1H phys damage plus leech), Sandcup Reprieve (5), Arena Thirst (4). The walks gave small leech nodes plus some Evasion Rating (unwanted for me) but also Damage. 38 points.
5. **Spurred Haste (8).** The road to it was 8 nodes: Life, Armour, Life, Damage, Life, 2 Attack Speed. Good walk. 46.
6. **Bleed.** Crimson Furrow (5, via DoT multiplier smalls), then Wound Dance (6, via 4 "Bleeding" small nodes at +25% each; the walk to the keystone is the best part of the keystone). Slow Red Rot (2). 59 points. Wound Dance's second line, "no extra Damage while moving", I could not judge in an auto-battler, so I took it with some doubt.
7. **Steadfast Tally (3), Triumph Fever (3).** I took them because they were cheap and give damage per charge, but I have no way to tell how I would gain charges beyond 5% on kill (low). 65.
8. **Lancepoint Charge (7; 15% phys, 5% attack speed, impale chance) and Torchbrand Wrath (3; 8% DoT multiplier).** Lancepoint's walk was Evasion, Armour, Strength, Damage, two phys nodes. Torchbrand's fire part is useless for me; I wanted only the DoT multiplier. 75.
9. **Red Gait (6; 10% more damage while leeching).** A "more" multiplier, which is the best kind of line here, and I had searched for it by `find "while Leeching"`. 81.
10. **Life.** I realised I had only 3 life notables available nearby. Campfire Resolve (10; 10% life + regen) and Bloodwell (9; 15% life, back across the hub in the Vanguard area, through Damage, Life Regeneration and Strength smalls). 100.

I did not refund anything. Things I considered and left: Spoils Draught (mana leech, wrong), Barbed Harrow (bow bleed only), Redsteel Kiss and Crimson Sluice (bleed chance, 14 and 15 points away in the Vanguard far north-west, and one is for swords), Thirsting Bellows (attack speed + leech, in far south of Vanguard region, cost 19).

## 3. The build I ended with

- 100/100 points. 17 notables, 1 keystone (Wound Dance), 70 small, 12 road/attribute nodes. By region: hub 6, strdex 85, str 9.
- Notables: Paired Fury, Hewing Rhythm, Sandthirst Hatchet, Whetted Hatchet, Sidearm Tithe, Sandcup Reprieve, Arena Thirst, Spurred Haste, Crimson Furrow, Slow Red Rot, Steadfast Tally, Triumph Fever, Lancepoint Charge, Torchbrand Wrath, Red Gait, Campfire Resolve, Bloodwell.
- Totals from `summary` (the ones that matter): 135% increased Damage with Axes; 147% increased DoT from Attack Skills with an Axe; 125% increased Damage with Bleeding; 14% DoT multiplier with Bleeding plus 21% general DoT multiplier; 23% increased Attack Speed plus 14% with Axes; 10% more damage while leeching; 1.5-1.6% of damage leeched as life and 100% increased leech speed; 80% increased maximum life, +90 Strength; bleeding stacks to 8.
- Score: **7/10**. Axes, bleeding, attack speed and leech are all present and substantial. Missing or weak: nothing on the tree gave me "chance to cause bleeding" for an axe (only +15% on a sword notable and a bow notable, and one in the far Vanguard west), so I do not know whether my bleeding will reliably apply; crit is not an issue; defences are thin (only life, some armour, no real block or mitigation); about 20 of the 100 points went into nodes I did not want (ES, resistances, evasion, fire, block, mana-less hub bits, plus two charge notables I am unsure about). The brief says "Vanguard", but the build is 85% Reaver region, and the Vanguard region added only Bloodwell.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree, mainly because `find`, `reach` and costs make it possible to plan, and the clusters (axes, leech, bleed) are grouped in one region so a theme feels real. The two biggest problems: (1) a Vanguard cannot see from the picture or the home region that the Reaver region is where his build lives, and the first walk is ES and resistances, and (2) bleed has no "chance to bleed" node, so the central mechanic of the fantasy feels unsupported. Best things: roads made of useful small nodes (axes, bleed, leech) so travel is never dead, and clear costs to each notable. Wound Dance's downside is unreadable.
