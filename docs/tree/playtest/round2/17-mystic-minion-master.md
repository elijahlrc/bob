# Playtest round 2, build 17: Mystic minion master

## 1. First impression from the pictures

The whole tree is a radial map with six wedges around the middle. Each wedge is a lattice of cells near the centre, then long thin roads out to far clusters at the rim. The Mystic wedge (top) has a honeycomb of cells directly above the start, long spurs to two far clusters (top-left and top-right), and a lot of unlabeled small clusters. The picture gave me no way to tell which cluster is "the minion one". The only minion keystone label I could see was Volatile Servants, top right, so I guessed "minions are far up-right". `find minion` showed there is a second minion area on the left, in the Zealot (strint) wedge, which no picture label hinted at.

The picture of the middle looks crowded and gives no hint of what the hub offers. The long grey spokes crossing the wedges look like decoration. Without `find`, I could not have planned this build from the images.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Find where minions live.** `find minion` gave 55 nodes. There are two main groups. The int group is top-right at about x 1000 to 1400, y -2700 to -3800. It has Marginal Familiars, Inkbound Servants, Moth Procession, Swift Scriveners, Moth Choir and the Volatile Servants keystone. The strint group is far left at about x -1000 to -1700, y -900 to -2300. It has Sacristan's Pact, Reliquary Mending, Ossuary Mender, Processional Haste, Tolling Congregation, Summoned Choir, Hurried Throng and Chorister's Eye. A single dexint notable, Understudy's Guard (minion block), costs 32 or more and sits at the east rim.
2. **int minion cluster first.** I took Marginal Familiars (#252) for 18 points. The walk went through about 14 small nodes (Mana, Spell Damage, Intelligence, Energy Shield). It passed no notable, but it did give me 40% mana, 20% ES and +40 int, which suits a Mystic. After arriving, the other notables were cheap: Inkbound 3, Moth Procession 4, Swift Scriveners 3, Moth Choir 4, Spiteful Footnote (+1 hex) 3, Volatile Servants 5. That is 21 points for 6 minion notables and a keystone, so the cluster felt dense and rewarding. Total 40 points.
3. **Energy shield.** The ES notables Mothwing Aegis (2), Cloaked Silence (4), Stormveil (4) and Thin Veil (4) were near the road I had already taken. That cost 14 points for 96% ES and 45% recharge rate in total. This worked well.
4. **Second minion cluster, strint.** I wanted more minion life and regeneration, so I allocated Reliquary Mending (#1805). It cost 19 points. The path ran through the middle and left of the start through attribute and Strength nodes, with a few Armour nodes on the way that I did not want. Once there, Sacristan's Pact 2, Sanctified Fervor 2, Processional Haste 2, Tolling Congregation 3, Ossuary Mender 4, Summoned Choir 3, Hurried Throng 3 and Chorister's Eye 2 were cheap.
5. **Leftovers.** Vellum Ward (4, ES) and Chalice Deep (2, mana) used up the last points. I did not find a good use for them. I had no ES-related unique reason to take mana, so these were filler.
6. **Hexes.** I searched for `hex`, `curse`, `hexed` and `mark`. Hexes are very thin. I found Spiteful Footnote (+1 hex, in the int minion cluster), Tolling Malison and some small "Effect of your Hexes" nodes in strint, and Hexbinder's Tithe, Hunter of the Marked and Whisper Network far away in dexint (cost 22 to 35). I took only Spiteful Footnote plus its two 5% hex effect nodes.

## 3. The build I ended with

100 of 100 points: 21 notables, 1 keystone, 63 small, 15 road. By region: int 58, hub 6, strint 36.

Keystone: Volatile Servants. Notables: Marginal Familiars, Inkbound Servants, Moth Procession, Swift Scriveners, Moth Choir, Spiteful Footnote, Mothwing Aegis, Cloaked Silence, Stormveil, Thin Veil, Reliquary Mending, Sacristan's Pact, Sanctified Fervor, Processional Haste, Tolling Congregation, Ossuary Mender, Summoned Choir, Hurried Throng, Chorister's Eye, Vellum Ward, Chalice Deep.

Totals from `summary`: 230% increased Damage of your Minions, 175% increased Life of your Minions, 136% increased Minion Attack and Movement Speed, 5 additional Minions, 15% additional Physical Damage Reduction for minions, minions mend 2% life per second, 18% increased minion area of effect, 15% reduced mana cost of minion skills, 130% increased maximum ES plus 28 flat, 55% ES recharge rate, 131% increased maximum mana, +120 int, 10% hex effect and 1 additional hex.

**Score: 8/10.** Minion damage, life, speed and count are very well covered, and ES is solid. It loses points because hexes are barely there (+1 hex and 10% effect), there is no minion block or resistance without a trip to the east rim, and my own life is low (5% increased maximum life, but I have ES). I also could not make any use of the Volatile Servants keystone judgement without knowing the minion skill.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree, because `find` and `reach` make a plan easy and the minion clusters are dense. The two biggest problems: hexes are almost missing near a Mystic (the minion and hex themes are not together), and the pictures do not show where each theme lives, so I needed `find` to discover the second minion cluster. The two best things: the int minion cluster behind a single 18-point walk, which also pays ES and mana on the way; and the ES notables lying along that road, so the walk costs little.
