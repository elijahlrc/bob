## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree reads as a star with six wedges. The six class starts sit in a tight ring in the middle, and each wedge is a big flower of clusters. The long straight grey spokes and the faint wedge borders make it easy to know which region is which.
- The labelled keystone discs far from the centre (Searing Avatar, Living Ward, Fever Pitch, Shade Leech, Hollow Vessel, Unerring Discipline and others) look like the "go on an expedition" goals. They are very far out. In the Zealot picture Living Ward is about 4300 units from the start.
- The Zealot region picture is dense and confusing: about 60 notables, all with similar mini names (Vesper X, Pilgrim's X, Ashen X). Names alone do not say what a notable gives, so I could not plan from the picture alone. I had to use `region` and `find`.
- I could see at a glance that a staff cluster exists: the long vertical spoke at top left of the Zealot picture (ids 1867 to 1900) has staff-flavoured names (Crozier, Crook, Rod). I could not tell this from names alone; only `find staff` confirmed it.
- The middle of the tree looks like a small ring with a few hub notables and keystones (Strongarm, Crimson Pact, Pain Conduit). It looks like a crossing, not a place to build. It looks nearly pointless for me.
- Many small dots and short road bridges make the picture look noisy. I could not tell which roads are worth walking.

## 2. Allocation log

1. Wanted: the staff nodes first. `find staff` found 7 notables and 18 small nodes: five in the Zealot region (Ironwood Penance 1876, Pilgrim's Crook 1875, Crozier's Verdict 1886, Sentinel Crozier 1900, Wandering Beacon 1889), two far away in the Mystic region (Orrery Crook 353, Lamplit Crook 356). I also found Vesper Rod 1868, Beadle's Aim 1867 and Anchorite's Stillness 1882, which do not say "staff" in the name.
2. Took Vesper Rod 1868 (staff attack speed and crit, cost 21). The walk was 20 nodes north-west. It passed through roughly 4 armour, 5 energy shield, 4 life, 3 mana, 3 strength and 2 intelligence nodes. These were on-theme for an ES plus armour character, so the walk was not wasted. But it felt like a long toll, and the first notable on the whole road is the destination.
3. Then, from there, the staff spoke was cheap: Ironwood Penance +3 (30% damage and crit with staves), Ashen Triptych 1851 +2 (elemental damage, resists, ailment chances), Beadle's Aim 1867 +4, Pilgrim's Crook 1875 +4, Anchorite's Stillness 1882 +4 (30% elemental damage with staves, penetration), Crozier's Verdict 1886 +4, Wandering Beacon 1889 +4. Total 46 points. Each had 3 to 4 smalls on the way giving staff crit or staff damage, so it felt like a real district.
4. Elemental damage: `find lightning` showed that the only lightning on my side is in the west Zealot arm (Stormglass Hymn 1839, Rimefrost Psalm 1935). The other lightning notables (Stormlamp Cant, Forkglass Lance, Stormglass Etching) are in the Mystic region at cost 25 and up. Fire is plentiful. Took Brimstone Hymn 1836 (+2), Smoldering Heresy 1835 (+3), Stormglass Hymn 1839 (+3), Kindling Penance 1832 (+3), Pyre Bloom 1825 (+3), Rimefrost Psalm 1935 (+3). The costs here were low because the western branch starts close to the staff road.
5. Rejected Searing Avatar (cost 7 at that point): "deal no non-Fire damage" fights with taking lightning. Rejected Prismatic Balance for the same kind of reason (it hurts elemental builds, I think).
6. Defence: Cinder Vestments 1907 (+7), Cloistered Plate 1917 (+4), Mailed Cassock 1914 (+2). These gave armour plus ES together, which was what I wanted. Roads in that direction carried a lot of armour.
7. Also Pilgrim's Stride 1984 (+2), Flagellant's Spark 1850 (+4, elemental damage with attacks, melee crit), Grudge Engine 2023 (+4), Sentinel Crozier 1900 (+4, staff block), Tallow Heart 1920 (+4), Marrow Hymn 1759 (+2), Silent Cloister 1781 (+3). One point left unspent.
8. I did not spend anything in the hub or other regions. Hub notables cost 7 to 21 and give generic ES, armour and life, which are a worse deal than the Zealot arm.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 99 of 100 points. 24 notables, 0 keystones, 63 small, 12 road/attribute.
- Key totals: 268% armour, 75% max ES, 78% max life, 66% max mana, +120 str, +70 int, 250% crit chance while wielding a staff (plus 100% with Staves), +81% crit multiplier while wielding a staff, 90% staff damage, 62% elemental damage with staves, 74% elemental damage, 85% fire damage, 85% lightning damage, 8% penetration with attacks, 20% all resist, 18% DoT multiplier, 5% of physical as extra fire and 5% as extra lightning.
- Score: 8/10. It does exactly what the brief said: a staff, fire and lightning, crit and both ES and armour. What costs it points: (a) I cannot tell what the staff attacks are (melee or spell), and half the elemental notables are spell-only (the conversion lines, Oracle's Knell, Keen Orison), so I skipped them; (b) crit multiplier is thin compared to crit chance (350% chance against about 100% multiplier), so the crit side is uneven; (c) lightning is shallow (two notables); (d) no keystone appealed.

## 5. Overall

Yes, I would enjoy planning on this tree for a staff build: the staff spoke is a real district and the road to it carries defence that suits ES plus armour. The two biggest problems: names and the picture do not tell you what a cluster is for, so you need the search tool; and the middle of the tree and the far regions give a staff elemental character nothing worth the walk. Crit multiplier and lightning are thin compared to crit chance and fire. The two best things: the staff district, and defence nodes that combine armour and ES.
