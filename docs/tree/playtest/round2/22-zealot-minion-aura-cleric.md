# Playtest round 2, build 22: Zealot minions-and-auras cleric

Build file: C:/Users/littl/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/E--Proj-Auto/cfb7adfb-b8bd-4736-8b0b-db97344ecab5/scratchpad/audit2/22.json (98 of 100 points spent).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big radial star. A small hub is in the middle with six start nodes, and six wedges run out from it.
- The spokes are long, thin grey roads. Each wedge is a landscape of small clusters (rings of dots with a ring notable), with a few big keystone discs far out on the rim.
- Easy to read: the keystones with big labels (Mana Bastion, Mind Bulwark, Volatile Servants, Living Ward, Searing Avatar, Blood Rite). I could tell at once that "minions" would be north-east of my start, in the int wedge (Volatile Servants is labelled there).
- The Zealot region picture is dense, with a lot of notable names (the 17xx to 20xx ids). I could not tell from the picture alone which cluster was the minion cluster and which the mana cluster. I had to use `find`.
- The Zealot start sits at the bottom right of its own region, so almost the entire wedge is "away" from the start. The picture gives no sense of how many points each excursion costs.
- Looks pointless: the middle hub. It looks like a ring of tiny dots with a few notables. Nothing in the hub pictures shouted "aura" or "minion". The upper part of the int wedge (Fever Pitch, wand and fire spell clusters) is plainly other builds' land.
- The long straight grey lines (for example Votive Buckler to Anchorite's Stillness) look like very long roads. I expected a lot of filler there.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Wanted: aura effect and reduced reservation.** I searched `find aura`, `find reserv`, `find reduced Mana Reserved`, `find aura effect` (0 hits) and `find reduced Reservation` (0 hits). The nearest hit was Thurible Hush (#1754, 6% reduced Mana Reserved, 10% aura effect), 7 points away in my own region at @-1387,5. It has four "Mana Reserved" smalls of 4% each around it. The road to it passed Armour, ES, Strength and ES smalls and the first two Mana Reserved smalls. I added the other two smalls (+2 points). Result: 22% reduced reservation, 13% aura effect for 9 points. This was the best moment of the build: a mini-package exactly on theme.
2. **Wanted: ES and mana, and life.** Silent Cloister (#1781, ES, mana, cost reduction), 3 points further, and Marrow Hymn (#1759, 8% life, 3% aura effect, +20 Str), 3 points. I added the cheap life and ES smalls beside them (2 points). Total 17 points.
3. **Wanted: minion nodes.** `find minion` showed a long list of minion notables in the north of the strint region (ids 1789 to 1814), all at cost 7 to 15, plus the "additional Minions" nodes in the int wedge (cost 25 to 26 then). Sacristan's Pact (#1778, mana, 20% minion damage, 15% reduced minion mana cost) was 4 away. I took it, then Chalice Deep (#1774) and Deep Well Chant (#1771), two 25% mana notables, with Slow Incense (#1767, mana regen and life regen). That was 18 points, so mana is now very strong.
4. **Minion cluster.** Sanctified Fervor (#1813) cost 6, but its road was Armour, Int, Armour, Int, Damage: 5 filler nodes and none of it was minion-related. From it I took Reliquary Mending, Chorister's Eye, Processional Haste, Tolling Congregation, Ossuary Mender and Summoned Choir. Each cost 2 to 4 points, and the roads between them had minion life, minion damage and minion speed smalls. The walk gave me something there. 35 points of minion stuff brought me to 52 used.
5. **Haloed Margin (#467, 30% aura AoE, 6% aura effect).** It was the only other aura notable. It cost 13 points. The walk went through the int wedge (a road of ES, mana, int and cast-speed smalls), so the walk was not wasted: it also put me on the road to Twin Wells, Tidepool Mind, Vellum Ward and Quiet Reservoir. But the notable itself is weak for me: aura AoE is useless to a cleric that wants its minions to be buffed, and the two AoE smalls next to it are dead. 65 points used.
6. **ES and mana notables** in the int wedge: Twin Wells (4), Tidepool Mind (5), Vellum Ward (4), Quiet Reservoir (2). 80 points used.
7. **Additional minions.** Moth Procession (#257, +3 minions) cost 12 from my build and Marginal Familiars (#252, +2 minions) cost 3 after that. The Moth Procession road ran through Spell Damage, Int, Mana, ES and Minion Speed smalls. 92 points.
8. **Last 6 points:** Marginal Familiars (3) and Stormveil (3, 18% ES). I left 2 points unspent because nothing useful cost 2 (Cloistered Ink and Brimming Inkwell were both 2: Brimming Inkwell is 40% damage while ES is full, which I could have taken; I judged it irrelevant for a minion build).

I considered the keystones Mana Bastion (ES protects mana, skill costs paid from ES) and Mind Bulwark (30% of damage from mana first). Both were within 9 to 10 points at the end. I declined them: Mana Bastion sounds like it could clash with reservation (I could not tell from the text how the two interact), and I did not have the points. Volatile Servants (minions burst at low life) was 6 away at the end. I did not want it.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 98 of 100 points. 22 notables, 0 keystones, 64 small, 12 roads. Regions: strint 55, int 42, hub 1.
- Notables: Thurible Hush, Silent Cloister, Marrow Hymn, Sacristan's Pact, Chalice Deep, Deep Well Chant, Slow Incense, Sanctified Fervor, Reliquary Mending, Chorister's Eye, Processional Haste, Tolling Congregation, Ossuary Mender, Summoned Choir, Haloed Margin, Twin Wells, Tidepool Mind, Vellum Ward, Quiet Reservoir, Moth Procession, Marginal Familiars, Stormveil.
- Key totals (from `summary`):
  - 254% increased max mana, 127% increased max ES, 41% increased max life.
  - 22% reduced Mana Reserved, 19% increased effect of Auras on you, 42% aura area.
  - 5 additional Minions, 150% minion damage, 100% minion life, 60% minion attack and move speed, minions mend 2%/s.
  - 70% mana regen, 3% life regen/s, 15% reduced minion mana cost.
- Score: **8 out of 10.**
  - Minions: complete and satisfying (count, damage, life, speed, mend).
  - Mana and ES: very strong.
  - Reservation: 22% reduced is decent.
  - Aura effect: 19%, from only 5 sources over the whole tree. It is the thin part.
  - Life: moderate (41%), and no minion defences beyond life (no minion resists, block or damage reduction within reach). I took only 2 minion-health-adjacent extras.
  - The build is clear and sensible, but the "strong aura support" part of the brief is under-served by the tree.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree. Finding things with `find` is fast, and roads in the minion and int districts give real value while walking. The two biggest problems: aura effect is thin (19% at most, and three of the eight aura notables are in distant wedges), and duplicated lines on notable cards ("1 additional Minions" three times) make the cards hard to add up. Also the first points near the Zealot start give caster builds nothing. The two best things: the Thurible Hush ring (reduced reservation plus aura effect, cheap and in my own wedge) and the strint minion district, where every road node is a minion node.

## Summary

Build 22 (Zealot minion and aura cleric) came out well at 8 out of 10. Minions and mana/ES are lush and cheap. Aura effect is thin: only 19% reachable, and the aura AoE notable was dead weight at 13 points. Duplicate lines on cards ("1 additional Minions" three times) are confusing. The walk to what I wanted mostly gave useful nodes; the hub gave nothing for this build.
