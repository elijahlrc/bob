# Playtest 24: Shade spellblade (dexint + int)

Build file (final): `scratchpad/audit2/24.json`. Alternative I tried first: `24-full.json` (dexint only, 100 pts, no refunds) and `24-alt.json` (same as final).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big radial web with six wedges. My start (Shade, upper right of the centre) sits in a wedge that is mostly a thin dotted fringe of small clusters, with long straight "roads" running outward and rows of small clusters hanging off them. Nothing says "go here for X": from the picture alone I could only find named things by their label.
- Labels in the whole picture show almost only keystones and a few notables. The Shade region picture is full of notable names, and a large share of them read as trap, mine, brand, claw, dagger, poison, damage-over-time. My first read was "this wedge is a rogue/trapper wedge". I could not see a spell or cast speed cluster. The first spell-flavoured name I noticed was "Needle Incantation", far to the right and top (x 3538).
- The int region (top) looks like a dense hexagon honeycomb in the middle and sprawling arms to the top. It looked like the place for spells, but the honeycomb makes it hard to tell where one cluster ends and another begins.
- Things that look pointless: the centre ("hub") ring around the class starts looks like a place of small notables only. The big gap between the Shade fringe and the int honeycomb (the area around the Mystic start) looked thin.
- Finding my way: I would not have found anything by picture alone. I used `find` for everything.

## 2. Allocation log

1. Wanted: spell damage, spell crit, cast speed, attack speed, one-hand weapon damage, mana, ES, dodge. Ran `reach` from the start. Everything within cost 8 was trap, mine, flask, strength/dex attribute notables, plus Crimson Pact. The only on-brief notables near the start were "Knife and Whisper" (cost 7: 12% attack damage, 12% spell damage, mana regen, +20 int), "Stoppered Reserve" (5: +25% mana), "Fleet Shadow" (9: 9% attack speed, 5% cast speed) and "Lone Hand Knack" (9: one-hand weapon damage). I took those four, 23 points total. The roads to them were small damage, mana, crit, attack speed and evasion nodes, which was mostly useful.
2. Searched `find "spell crit"`, `find "critical strike chance for spells"` (0 hits), then `find "Critical Strike Chance with Spells"` (6 notables, 12 small). The first two wordings fail only because the real text is "with Spells". Nearest spell crit in my own wedge: "Needle Incantation" (dexint, far right at x 3538, cost 22). Other spell crit notables were int (north, cost 32-33) or strint (west, 29-30).
3. Took Sparkwick Trance (+5 for cast speed and crit multi) and Compass of Marrow in the hub (+6, 16% damage, 4% attack speed): 34 points.
4. Decided to walk east to Needle Incantation (cost 20, 54 total). The walk was long but decent: evasion, ES, dex, int, damage, crit chance smalls the whole way, then two spell crit smalls just before the notable. Then Keyhole Glimpse, Hush Before Cut, Whetted Malice (cost 8 together, each 2 to 4 off the same road), Taintedsteel Pace (8), Masked Precision, Dim Wellspring, Fogbound Ward, Nimble Hush (3 to 4 each), Gossamer Dusk (3), Spellslip (7, the road to it gave four cast speed smalls, nice), Veilstep (2), Mended Mask (4), Sipped Wick (3): 100 points.
5. The summary showed only 12% increased spell damage (from Knife and Whisper alone). I had 295% increased crit chance and 134% damage, but nothing that says "spells". Having assumed the spell damage lived in the int region, I checked `find "Spell Damage"`: the smalls (7% each) are in int, and the cheapest ones were 8 steps from my build.
6. Tried unallocating Sipped Wick, Mended Mask, Veilstep, Gossamer Dusk, Spellslip. `unalloc` gave back one point per node; the road nodes leading to them stayed allocated (97 then 95 points used). I stopped there; I did not hunt for each road node.
7. Started a second build from the start (`24-alt.json`), going straight north into int to the spell cluster: "Comet's Edge" (cost 33 from the start, 50% spell crit and 15 multi), "Comet Script" (+3), "Starlit Cantrip" (+2), "Archivist's Trance" (+3), "Needle-Star Glint" (+5), "Glyphwright's Draught" (+3), "Lampwick Reservoir" (+3), "Halo Script" (+4). 56 points for eight spell notables. The 33-point walk gave Spell Damage, Cast Speed, mana, ES and int smalls the whole way, with no filler I would call wasted. Then I added the dexint basics back: Knife and Whisper, Compass of Marrow, Fleet Shadow, Lone Hand Knack, Sparkwick Trance, plus Knife-Edge Gloss (+40 crit multi), Cloaked Silence, Thin Veil, Stormveil, Drip-Lamp Cistern. Total 100 points. This became the final build.

## 3. The build I ended with (alt build, `24.json`)

- 100/100 points: 18 notables, 0 keystones, 67 small, 15 road. By region: hub 12, int 68, dexint 20.
- Notables: Comet's Edge, Comet Script, Starlit Cantrip, Archivist's Trance, Needle-Star Glint, Glyphwright's Draught, Lampwick Reservoir, Halo Script, Knife-Edge Gloss, Knife and Whisper, Compass of Marrow, Fleet Shadow, Lone Hand Knack, Sparkwick Trance, Cloaked Silence, Thin Veil, Stormveil, Drip-Lamp Cistern.
- Totals from `summary`: 173% spell damage, 72% damage, 155% spell crit chance + 40% generic crit, +27% spell crit multi + 79% crit multi, 36% cast speed, 19% attack speed (+8% one-hand melee), 147% max mana, 116% max ES, 25% ES recharge, 45% mana regen, +230 int, 20% one-hand damage, no dodge, 24% evasion.
- Score: 7/10. Spell damage, spell crit, cast speed, mana and ES are all strong. Attack speed and one-hand weapon notables are thin (a spellblade wants crit chance on the weapon hit; Lone Hand Knack is physical/melee flavoured). No dodge at all (Spellslip lives at the far right edge and costs about 30 points to reach from the north cluster). The "straddling" is really "walk through dexint's thin fringe and basically live in int".
- The first, dexint-only build (`24-full.json`, 100 points): crit chance 295% (generic), 90% spell crit, 35% cast speed, 38% attack speed, 97% ES, 30% spell dodge, but only 12% spell damage. Score 5/10: very crit-rich but nothing in it makes spells hit harder; it reads like a generic crit build.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree once I knew where to go, but a Shade spell player starts with nothing for spells nearby: the first spell crit is 22 points east or 33 north, and the picture does not say so. Two biggest problems: spell damage and spell crit are missing from the Shade fringe (far too many trap/mine notables there), and dodge is far from the ES roads. Two best things: the roads give what the build wants (the north walk is almost all spell damage, cast speed, mana and ES), and the Comet cluster in int is a satisfying destination. The straddle was not a straddle: the dexint half of the build is a thin hub of weapon basics and the int half is the build.
