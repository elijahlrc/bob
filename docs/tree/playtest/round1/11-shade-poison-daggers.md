# Playtest 11: Shade, poison-and-chaos dagger fighter

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/11.json`. 99 of 100 points spent, all in the dexint region.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- **Whole tree.** It reads as a galaxy: a tight hub in the middle with six class starts, then six wedges, each a loose web of small clusters joined by thin lines. It is big and pretty. At whole-tree size I cannot tell what anything does. Only the 20-odd named keystones, with their coloured discs, stand out.
- **My region (dexint, upper right).** It is crowded.
  - Notable names overlap in the picture (Masked Precision / Lone Hand Knack / Sparkwick Trance, Hexbinder's Tithe / Dim Wellspring and others are unreadable). I could not pick a route from the picture alone.
  - The region has recognisable strands: a wide band of notables across the top, a poison and DoT patch to the east, and dagger and claw notables to the north. The names are on theme (Stiletto, Talon, Widow's Vial, Nightshade). Naming is a real strength; I guessed "daggers up north, poison east" from names alone and I was right.
  - The "Strider" dex picture was not my brief, but the whole picture shows a big lower-right wedge that looked like the natural home of dex poison. That turned out to be a trap (see friction 1).
- **What looks pointless.**
  - Trap and mine notables fill the whole west half of my wedge (Gaping Pitfall, Hairpin Trigger, Warren of Snares and so on). They are the nearest notables to Shade start and nothing for a melee build.
  - Bow-only and shield-only notables sit on the main road.
  - Mana notables are everywhere.
- **Navigation plan.** Use `region dexint` for the list and `reach` for costs. The picture is only good for finding which direction is which.

## 2. Allocation log

Costs are points paid, including the road.

1. **Wanted** poison and DoT multiplier first, as the core. `find poison` returned 29 nodes. The best-looking notables (Hemlock Draught, Nightshade Talon, Venomed Fletching, Adder's Kiss, Thornneedle Dance) are in the **dex** region, shown with cost `-`. `alloc` on them said "cannot be reached". I tried two other dex nodes (Ridge Archer's Pace and Thornneedle Dance) with the same result. So a Shade cannot get the dex dagger-poison cluster at all, or not within 100 points. The tool does not say which.
2. **Widow's Vial (#562), 17 points.** It was the first dexint poison notable. The road was 9 smalls plus attribute nodes. I got +60% crit chance, ES, evasion, Dex and Int and 4 small DoT-multiplier nodes. I did not plan to want those; most of the road was useful. This was the most expensive single step, and it opened up everything else.
3. **Rotwhisper Cant (#717, 5), Lingering Rue (#571, 5), Gloomblight Draught (#579, 3).** All of them chaos and DoT multiplier. The notables cost 3 to 5 each from Widow's Vial, which felt good. The roads are mostly "DoT multiplier" smalls, so the road is on theme.
4. **Cruel Agony (#745, 5).** The road went through four "Poison Damage" smalls (25% each). The keystone makes ailments scale with crit multiplier, so it is the point of a crit-poison build. Easy to find because the keystone is named in the picture.
5. **Slow Fester (#576, 2), then Slow Nightshade (#570, 3).** Nightshade is written for bows, and about 40% of its value (bow damage, bow DoT) is dead for me. I took it for the DoT multiplier, +10% duration, poison speed and +20% poison chance on the bow smalls (the only poison-chance nodes I could reach). It was a bad deal, but there was no alternative.
6. **Taintedsteel Pace (#845, 8).** Physical-to-chaos extra and attack speed; the road had DoT multiplier smalls and ES. The cost was higher than the picture implies. It is near the trap and mine strand; I found it only because `reach` listed it.
7. **Dagger strand: Pinpoint Stiletto (#631, 8), Needlepoint Verdict (#637, 5), Hungering Stiletto (#627, 4), Stiletto Rhythm (#632, 5).** The dagger roads share nodes, so each next notable is cheap. This is the best-designed part of what I saw.
8. **Masked Precision (#809, 5).** Generic damage and crit, not weapon-locked. Fine.
9. **Defence: Fogbound Ward (#691, 11).** 15% evasion, 10% ES and recharge, resists. The road was 10 nodes (Dex, evasion, ES, damage). It was expensive, but it was the first notable that mixes evasion and ES, and the one I wanted.
10. **Fleet Shadow (#808, 6).** +9% attack speed. Most of the cost was the road (attack speed small, crit, Dex).
11. **Gossamer Dusk (#699, 3) and Fevered Nick (#800, 3), plus one Max Life small (#802).** Life and ES, ending the build with life, evasion and ES. The previous point's reach list showed these at cost 3 each, which was the best price paid for anything. I left 1 point unspent.

I did not regret anything enough to refund it.

## 3. The build I ended with

- Points: 99 of 100. 16 notables, 1 keystone, 58 small, 24 road/attribute.
- Notables: Widow's Vial, Rotwhisper Cant, Lingering Rue, Gloomblight Draught, Slow Fester, Slow Nightshade, Taintedsteel Pace, Pinpoint Stiletto, Needlepoint Verdict, Hungering Stiletto, Stiletto Rhythm, Masked Precision, Fogbound Ward, Fleet Shadow, Gossamer Dusk, Fevered Nick.
- Keystone: Cruel Agony.
- Totals (key ones from `summary`):
  - Damage over Time: +71% increased DoT, +50% to DoT multiplier (40 and 10), plus +14% to Poison DoT multiplier.
  - Poison: 130% increased Damage with Poison, +10% poison duration, +10% poison speed, only +20% chance to Poison on Hit.
  - Chaos: +82% increased Chaos Damage, +4% of Physical as extra Chaos, +38% Chaos Resistance.
  - Daggers: +104% Damage with Daggers, +152% Crit Chance with Daggers, +78% Crit Multiplier with Daggers, +80% DoT with attacks while wielding a dagger.
  - Crit: +180% increased Crit Chance on top.
  - Speed: 16% Attack Speed and 18% with Daggers.
  - Defence: +162% Evasion, +55% ES, +23% Life, regeneration 1.5% Life per second, +16% all Elemental Resistances.
  - Attributes: +150 Dex, +110 Int.
- **Score: 7/10.**
  - It does what the brief says: dagger crit, poison and chaos, and attack speed, with a decent evasion and ES mix.
  - It loses points because poison application is thin, with only +20% chance to poison on hit (from bow smalls). A poison build without a chance-to-poison notable is in a bad way. The best poison dagger notables were out of reach.
  - Chaos Resistance +38% is cheap and nice, but the build has no way to go with Hollow Vessel or a chaos-immunity plan.
  - About 10 points are spent on nodes with dead text (bow lines).

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree; the strands have identities and the `reach` costs are readable. The two biggest problems: the dex poison and dagger cluster is unreachable for a Shade, and chance to poison on hit is missing from dagger paths. Names mislead little but the picture is unreadable at dexint scale. The two best things: the dagger strand is cheap to walk once you are in it, and the road nodes are mostly on theme (DoT multiplier, poison damage, crit), so passing through is not a waste.
