# Playtest 06: Mystic who wants to be an armoured axe/mace fighter

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/06.json`. 98 of 100 points spent. Tool calls used: about 30.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-armed flower. A small bright hub sits in the middle with the six class starts (Mystic top, Zealot upper left, Shade upper right, Strider lower right, Reaver bottom, Vanguard lower left) on a ring around it. Each arm is a big cluster of districts.
- Landmarks are easy: the coloured keystone discs with names (Mana Bastion, Mind Bulwark, Blood Rite, Rooted Stance...) read well from far away. Within a region I could tell "where the notables are" at a glance.
- Strength is the lower-left arm (Vanguard). The notable names there are about what I want: Stonehewn Brawn, Monolith Skin, Roadworn Plate, Quarryman's Sweep (axes), Crowning Mallet and Mortar Knell (maces), Forgehand (melee damage). My first plan was "walk from the Mystic start through the hub into the lower-left arm, about 15 to 20 points, and live there".
- The Mystic arm (top) is a huge area, full of ES, mana, minion, spell and wand/staff names. Nothing in it looks like armour or melee. Its far top is a long way from the hub.
- Looks pointless: large stretches of the Mystic arm are blue/purple dots, which are mana and ES. For my character that is half the map. The hub ring has many small "Strength" road nodes that I expected to be a bridge.
- I assumed the hub joined all six arms, since it is drawn in the middle with spokes.

## 2. Allocation log

1. `new mystic --budget 100`, `legend`. Fine.
2. `reach 6` showed the nearest notables: Brimming Inkwell (ES-full damage), Mirror Folio, Wide Constellation, then Crossroads Bones in the hub at 7 points. Nothing for me nearby, as expected.
3. `region str` listed 53 notables. The Cost column was "-" for every one. I took that as "too far to price", so I tried `alloc 1599` (Stonehewn Brawn). Result: "cannot be reached", 0 points spent.
4. Debugging the wall (about 8 calls). I tried: Runecut Slate, Quickforge Hand, Stake and Mallet (the nearest Str notables), the first small after the Vanguard start (#1782), and the Vanguard start itself (#4). All "cannot be reached". I allocated the hub road #77 that touches the Vanguard start (16 points), then tried #4 again from there: still "cannot be reached". I refunded #77. The hub nodes next to the Zealot start and the other starts are the same. `reach 25` showed that notables in strint, dexint, dex, strdex and str all have cost "-". Only the hub and the Mystic's own int region can be priced. `near` still draws the whole graph, so the picture and `near` both show the Vanguard arm as connected. The tool just refuses to enter it.
   - My conclusion: another class's start node is a closed gate, and every other region is only joined to the hub through its start node. So a Mystic cannot spend one point outside int and hub. The brief says the tree should show what happens to someone who crosses. The answer is that you cannot cross.
5. Re-plan: take the best "tank-like" stuff in the hub and in int. `find` for Melee, Mace, Axe, Physical, Armour in int and hub: no melee nodes, no mace or axe nodes (the only "Axe" hit was "Waxen Idol", a totem), no physical damage except wand ones, and exactly one armour notable (Shared Sorrow in the hub). Block-attack nodes exist only for dual wielding (Twin Lanterns) and shields give spell block.
6. Hub: allocated Pilgrim's Rest (6 points, via two life smalls), Shared Sorrow (6, passing three Armour and Evasion smalls and a Damage small), Compass of Marrow (9, passing life/damage smalls), Wanderer's Tithe (9, resistances, life), Common Grave (6), Hollow Heart (6). Total 57 points, hub done: I now had every hub notable that matters. Left over in the hub: Strongarm (projectile-attack Str bonus, useless in melee), Crimson Pact (instant leech but no life regeneration, no leech source in reach, and it conflicts with Common Grave), Pain Conduit (spells). Skipped all three.
7. Int: Heavy Folio (+30 Str) cost 11 and went through six Intelligence roads, which gave +60 Int I do not want. Mirrored Hearth (+3, life and resistance). Twin Lanterns (+3, dual wield: block attacks, 6% attack speed). Twin Wells (+7, 8% life, six Intelligence roads on the way). Mirror Folio (+3, spell block with a shield). Mind Bulwark keystone (+9; 30% of damage from mana first; also Int roads and four Mana smalls on the way). Marrow Veil (+5, chaos resistance, ES).
8. Stopped at 98. The last two points had nothing worth taking.

## 3. The build I ended with

- Points: 98 of 100. By region: hub 57, int 41. By kind: 13 notables, 1 keystone (Mind Bulwark), 44 small nodes, 40 road/attribute nodes.
- Notables: Crossroads Bones, Pilgrim's Rest, Shared Sorrow, Compass of Marrow, Wanderer's Tithe, Common Grave, Hollow Heart, Heavy Folio, Mirrored Hearth, Twin Lanterns, Twin Wells, Mirror Folio, Marrow Veil.
- Totals from `summary`: +180 Str, +230 Int, +30 Dex, 80% increased maximum Life, 60% increased maximum ES, 68% increased maximum Mana, 32% increased Armour (and 32% Evasion), 51% increased Damage (generic), +25% all elemental resistances, +20% chaos resistance, 4% attack speed (plus 6% while dual wielding), +10% attack block while dual wielding, +10% spell block with a shield, 30% of damage taken from mana first, 0.4% life regeneration per second.
- Match with the brief: **2 / 10**. Life is genuinely decent (80% increased). Armour is a joke (32%, all of it from one hub notable and three smalls, with an equal 32% to Evasion that I do not want). I have 0% melee damage, 0% axe or mace damage, no physical damage, no crit, no leech, and no bleed. The 51% "increased Damage" and 4% attack speed are the only damage for a melee character. +230 Int is wasted, and 60% ES is mostly wasted. The Mind Bulwark and Mirror Folio choices are "least bad", not "good".
- The honest answer to the brief: the tree does not treat a crosser badly in the usual sense (a long detour, weak nodes on the way). It does not allow it at all. A Mystic and a Vanguard are two different games on this tree.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning a Mystic build on this tree: the hub and the int arm are readable and the notables say what they do. The off-class brief fails completely. The two biggest problems: the other arms are locked behind their start nodes, so a Mystic cannot reach any melee or armour node; and nothing on the picture or the "cost -" output says so, so the player plans an impossible route. What worked: the hub mini-tree is solid and useful for any class, and the keystone and notable names and colours made the regions easy to read from far away.
