# Playtest 07: Reaver, sword and shield, blocks, bleeds, attacks fast

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/07.json`. 99 of 100 points spent. I only looked at the pictures `whole.png` and `region-strdex.png` before using the tool. I did not open the other five regional pictures, because the brief only needed my own region.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree looks like a snowflake. A small hub with the six class starts sits at the centre. Six arms run out to the regions, and each arm is a mess of small orange, grey and blue dots with rings (notables) scattered through it.
- Reading it as a whole is hard. The labelled big nodes (keystones such as Shieldwall, Plated Hide, Wound Dance, Nimble Gambit) are easy to spot because they have text next to them. The regional pictures help a lot, since every notable has a name and id.
- The Reaver region (bottom) is huge and has distinct neighbourhoods: a lot of weapon-type notables (axes, two-handers, maces, swords) in the upper part near the start, a shield and armour block in the lower left, mana nodes in the middle, bleed/poison on the right, totem/aura nodes in the lower right, and three keystones at the rim.
- I could find my way by weapon name in the labels. But the picture shows 61 notables and tells you nothing about cost. For example, Shieldwall Drill looks like a short walk from the start and costs 22 points from scratch.
- Things that look pointless:
  - The faint straight lines dividing the regions and the long chains of grey dots that run for ages. They look like decoration until you realise they are the roads you pay for.
  - Totem, aura and mana notables for a melee character.
  - The big hexagon lattices (lower left, lower middle). They hold lots of Armour/Evasion/Life small nodes but no label, so you cannot plan around them.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Learned the legend, ran `reach 8`.** I found only one notable in range (Tactician's Map, +30 Intelligence, cost 4) and Paired Fury / Feast of Frenzy at 8. A first look at what is near the start is disappointing for a fighter: the closest notable is Intelligence.
2. **Ran `region strdex`, plus `find` for Block, Bleed, Shield and Sword.** This is the real planning step. Everything I wanted exists, but at 15 to 26 points from the start: shield cluster (22 to 24, keystone 26), sword notables (11 and 12), bleed (16 and 17, keystone 20). The Block search returned 26 notables, most of them in other regions that turned out to be unreachable (see friction 1).
3. **Took Mailsplitter Cut (#1263), cost 11.** It passes through 5 roads and smalls (Armour, Strength x3, Evasion, Dex, Life). That is a lot of attribute for one sword notable, but the notable itself is good: sword damage, attack speed, armour penetration.
4. **Paired Fury (#1287), cost 3 once I had the roads, and Riposte Edge (#1441), cost 3.** Paired Fury is the best deal I found on the tree: 50% increased attack damage and 20% attack speed, both unconditional. Only its DoT line is dual-wield only. The road to it ran through two "+3% block while dual wielding" smalls, which I do not use. Riposte Edge is sword crit, and cheap because it sits next to Mailsplitter Cut.
5. **Rampart Windfall (#1354), cost 15 from my build.** This is the first block notable. The path was 12 nodes, mostly Strength, Dex and Evasion roads, then two "Defences from your Shield" smalls. After that, Shieldwall Drill cost 3, Bulwark Vow 3 and Tower Shield Rank 4. The shield cluster is excellent value once I got in: 4 notables for 25 points, +11% block, +115% attack damage while holding a shield, 20% attack speed. It was a good feeling to find that the cluster pays for its road.
6. **Spurred Haste (4), Sidearm Tithe (3), Campfire Resolve (5), Mailed Dancer (3).** Attack speed, 1H leech and phys, life and regen, armour and resistances.
7. **Shieldwall keystone (#1479), cost 10.** The path had four "Block Chance" smalls, so +10% max block is not the only thing it pays. I wanted the cap raised since I was stacking block. Cost: 30% less Evasion, which clashes with the 120% evasion I picked up from roads and Mailed Dancer. I accepted that.
8. **Lancepoint Charge (8).** Phys damage and attack speed plus impale chance.
9. **Bleed: Crimson Furrow (#1402, cost 11), Slow Red Rot (2), Wound Dance (5).** The road to Crimson Furrow was 8 nodes of roads and life/armour smalls. Wound Dance makes bleeding stack 8 times, which only matters if something applies bleeding. I found nothing reachable that does (see friction 2).
10. **Finished with Rivetbreaker (2), Gambeson Mantle (2), Red Gait (3).** One point left over, which no notable fit.
11. **Mistake I made and fixed.** Allocating a hub notable (Wanderer's Tithe, #61) by pointing `alloc` at it spent 9 points on a road I did not want. I took it back with `unalloc`. `unalloc` refuses to remove a node that others depend on, so I had to remove the road nodes from the far end first (the error "not taken, or others depend on it" does not say which). I also tried to reach nodes in the Vanguard (str) and Strider (dex) regions to get "chance to cause Bleeding" and sword-and-shield notables, and every attempt said "cannot be reached".

## 3. The build I ended with

99/100 points. 17 notables, 2 keystones (Shieldwall, Wound Dance), 47 small, 33 road/attribute nodes. So a third of my points went into roads.

Totals from `summary`:

- Block: +11% attack block while holding a shield, +4% block, +10% maximum block. (+6% dual-wield block, which I cannot use.) 225% increased Defences from your Shield.
- Offence: 115% increased attack damage (and DoT) while holding a shield, 50% increased attack damage, 20% attack speed, plus 20% attack speed while holding a shield and 20% more from other sources. 34% damage with swords, 40% sword crit chance. Armour penetration of 15%.
- Bleed: 14% DoT multiplier with bleeding, 125% increased damage with bleeding, 15% faster bleeding, +20% bleed duration, 8 stacks.
- Defence: 140% armour, 120% evasion (but 30% less from the keystone), 35% max life, regen 1.6%, +22% all resistances.
- Attributes: +190 Strength, +160 Dexterity.

Brief match: **7/10.**

- It blocks a lot and attacks fast. The block ceiling is raised and the shield cluster boosts damage while holding a shield.
- The sword is decently served (swords: damage, attack speed, crit).
- It bleeds only on paper. I have the multipliers, the duration and the stacks, but I found no way to cause bleeding on hit within reach. That is the one big hole.
- It spent 99 points without leaving the Reaver region, so it felt like a build the region forced on me rather than one I designed. The block total is lower than I wanted: I hit only about 15% base block from nodes.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree, because the shield cluster, the weapon names and `find` make a fighter easy to design. The biggest problems: the neighbouring regions, with the best sword and shield and bleed nodes for my build, cannot be reached and the tool does not say why; and I found no way to apply bleeding. A third of my points went to roads that I did not choose. The best things: the shield cluster pays back its entry road, and Paired Fury is a bargain. The listing of the path after each `alloc` is also clear.
