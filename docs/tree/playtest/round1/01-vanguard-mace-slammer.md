# Playtest 01: Vanguard, two-handed mace slammer

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/01.json` (100 of 100 points). A second scratch file (`01-probe.json`) was used only to test whether other regions can be entered; it is not part of the build.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a six-lobed flower around a small hub. Each lobe is a class region with its own colour mix. It is very big: thousands of nodes, and from the whole picture I cannot read a single node. The class-start labels and the ~30 big keystone labels at the rim are the only landmarks.
- My own region (str, lower left) reads as two things: a honeycomb of hexagonal ring roads close to the start (grey dots, many +10 Strength roads, a few notables) and, far out, a sprawling tangle of clusters. The notable names are very much in a theme ("Anvil Sunder", "Tollbell Quake", "Gravemaul Sway"): I could guess the mace and two-handed material would be in the south-west edge ("Crowning Mallet 1671", "Mortar Knell 1676", "Doubled Dirge 1667") and the life notables closer to the start (Bloodwell, Marrow Reservoir, Ossuary Heart). That guess was right, which is good.
- Looks pointless or confusing: the honeycomb next to the start has almost no notables, so it looks like pure travel. Several far keystones (Rooted Stance, Idle Hands, Unerring Discipline, Blood Rite) sit at the very rim, 17 to 36 points away, with a thin line of dots leading to each. Labels collide in the region picture (Blood Rite / Hearthfire Vigil, Thirsting Bellows / Stored Fortitude, Silent Cloister / Cairnlight Well), so some names are unreadable.
- Navigation plan: use the `region str` list (nearest first) as the shopping list, read the costs, and ignore the pictures except for the layout.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Wanted:** the mace and two-handed damage. `region str` showed two mace/2H clusters at the bottom left (about x -2400 to -3000, y 2100 to 3000), with the nearest notable "Crowning Mallet" at cost 29. Taking it was a long walk: 29 points, of which 19 were +10 Strength roads, 3 Armour, 1 Life Regeneration, 1 Strength; one notable at the end. Passed through: no notables on the way, but several life and armour notables sat 2 to 3 steps off the road (see below).
2. **Mace/2H cluster.** Once at Crowning Mallet the rest of the cluster was cheap: Anvil Sunder (3), Bloodtithe Maul (5), Gravemaul Sway (3), Doubled Dirge (5), Tollbell Quake (5), Mortar Knell (5), Forgehand (4, 30% melee damage). Each notable sits behind two small nodes of its own theme (Physical Damage with Maces / Two Handed Weapons), which reads very clearly. Total at this point: 57 points. Mace and 2H bonuses stack for a two-handed mace, which is a good design (218% mace physical, 151% 2H physical).
3. **Stun.** I searched for stun. Found only: Shatterbone Toll (10% reduced enemy stun threshold, grit charge on stun), Mossgrave Rest (my own stun threshold), Rooted Stance (keystone, cannot be stunned, cannot evade, cost 32 more). Took Shatterbone Toll (3), but its path is two "Grit Charge Duration" smalls that do nothing for me. 60 points.
4. **Tank, near the road home.** Taking more notables on the south branch: Tankard Mail (3), Ossuary Heart (3), Bulwark of Habit (3, block and grit charges; low value for me). 69 points.
5. **Tank, near the start.** On the way back up, Bloodwell (2), Stonehewn Brawn (2), Monolith Skin (2), Quickforge Hand (3), Ironbound Oath (3). All of these were 2 to 3 points because they sit right next to the road I had already bought. 81 points. This was the best moment of the session: the road bought for the damage cluster paid for the whole defensive build.
6. **More life.** Marrow Reservoir (3), Mossgrave Rest (3), Tombhearth Blood (3, +20 life, 10% life), Granite Sinew (3, Strength). 93 points.
7. **Last points.** Roadworn Plate (4: armour, +12% all resistances, movement speed), Stake and Mallet (2: impale). 99 points. The last point went on a "Physical Damage with Two Handed Weapons" small (1662).
8. **Probing.** I wanted more slam and stun choices, so I looked at other regions: the strdex (Reaver, bottom) region next door has "Headsman's Weight", "Both-Hands Oath", "Cudgel Cadence", and the strint region has "Bellstrike Rite" (60% mace crit, insight charge on stun). None can be reached from a Vanguard at any budget: `alloc` says "cannot be reached", even with 400 points, because the other classes' start nodes act as walls. The only non-str nodes a Vanguard can reach are the ten hub notables and keystones.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 100 of 100 points: 23 notables, 0 keystones, 50 small, 27 road nodes. All in the str region.
- Notables: Crowning Mallet, Anvil Sunder, Mortar Knell, Tollbell Quake, Bloodtithe Maul, Gravemaul Sway, Doubled Dirge, Forgehand, Quickforge Hand, Ironbound Oath, Stake and Mallet, Shatterbone Toll, Tankard Mail, Ossuary Heart, Bulwark of Habit, Bloodwell, Marrow Reservoir, Tombhearth Blood, Stonehewn Brawn, Monolith Skin, Roadworn Plate, Mossgrave Rest, Granite Sinew.
- Totals (from `summary`): +470 Strength, 105% increased maximum Life (+56 flat), 182% increased Armour (+276 flat), +24% all elemental resistances (+1% max), 5% additional physical damage reduction, 5% life regeneration per second; 218% increased physical damage with maces, 151% with two-handed weapons, 56% melee damage, 80% mace crit chance (+25% multiplier), 44% of enemy physical damage reduction removed, 4% double damage, 12% area, about 13% attack speed (5.5% melee, 5% 2H, 3% general), +15% impale chance, 10% reduced enemy stun threshold, my own stun threshold +20%.
- **Score: 7/10.** The damage and life/armour halves of the brief are served well and the numbers look right for a slammer. What is missing is the middle of the brief: nothing in my reach makes stuns bigger or more frequent (only a 10% lower enemy stun threshold), no "heavy stun", no stun duration, no slam/area scaling beyond 12%. Also 29 points were spent walking, and about 10 on grit-charge nodes that have no payoff in my build.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree, but only inside my own region. The two biggest problems: other regions are completely closed to a Vanguard (five-sixths of the tree is scenery, and the stun/mace nodes I wanted sit there), and there is no stun-power in reach for a "stun" build, plus a 29-point road that gives one notable. The two best things: the clusters read well (themed smalls leading to a notable, mace and 2H stacking), and the notables sitting a couple of points off the long road make the walk pay for the whole defensive half of the build.
