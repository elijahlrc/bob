# Playtest 08: Reaver, totem and warcry build

Build file: `C:/Users/littl/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/E--Proj-Auto/cfb7adfb-b8bd-4736-8b0b-db97344ecab5/scratchpad/audit/08.json` (100/100 points).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a six-armed star with a small hub in the middle. The class starts sit in a ring around it. Each arm is a big blob of clusters, joined by thin lines. Some arms are tidy (the hexagon honeycomb at the top, the rectangular grid in the upper right), and some are loose scatter (my own bottom arm).
- Finding my way: the Reaver start is at the bottom of the hub, and my region picture is the bottom arm. Labelled keystones sit at the ends of long, dotted spokes (Plated Hide, Shieldwall, Lone Vow). Those are easy to spot and read as destinations. Notable names with a number are readable, but the labels overlap badly in places (Hewing Rhythm and Spoils Draught, Torchbrand Wrath and Lancepoint Charge, Sidearm Tithe and Red Gait).
- The whole picture gives me no way to tell which cluster is about what. There is no visible theme geography. Totems, shields, bows and axes are all mixed through the same arm. I can only learn this by reading names.
- Looks pointless: the many tiny orange/yellow weapon-specific clusters (Axes, Maces, Swords, Bows, Two Handed). They look the same at a glance and I will never use most of them.
- I also assumed I could cross into the Vanguard arm (strength, lower left) for life nodes, because the Reaver is str/dex. That turned out to be false (see friction 1).

## 2. Allocation log

1. **What I wanted:** totem damage first, then life and armour. **Found:** `find totem` gives 14 notables and keystones across the whole tree, but only three are reachable by a Reaver (Roadside Effigy, Quickraised Pennant, Warstandard Muster). All three are in the far south-east and south of my arm, 22 to 23 points from the start. I tried `find warcry`, `shout`, `exert`, `rally`, `taunt` and `ancestral`. Nothing exists. The nearest things are "Marching Drumbeat" (auras) and "Banner Advance" (Fortified).
2. **Roadside Effigy (23 points).** Nothing nearby is a notable, so this is a road. The walk is 23 nodes through armour, strength, dexterity, max life and a few "Damage" smalls, and ended with 36% armour, 20% max life and 70 strength. This was a good surprise. The road is not wasted, it already is my defence.
3. **Quickraised Pennant (3 more)** and **Warstandard Muster (4 more)**. Both cluster off the road with totem smalls. I added one more totem small (1430) on the way. Subtotal 31 points with 106% totem damage, 66% cast speed and 30% attack speed for totems.
4. **Searched for an "additional totem" node.** The only one is the Idle Hands keystone in the Vanguard arm. I tried to alloc it, and also Bloodwell and Monolith Skin from a fresh 300-point test build. All gave "cannot be reached". `near` shows these nodes connect only via the Vanguard start (#4), which seems to be a wall. So the Reaver can only use the strdex arm plus a small hub. I deleted the test build.
5. **Defence run west, 63 points (Brigandine Stride 12 points, Gambeson Mantle 4, Veteran's Sinew 5, Plated Hide 6, Campfire Resolve 5).** It is one long road through armour and attributes, with notables strung along it. Plated Hide (evasion to armour) is a good fit for a Reaver, because my roads give plenty of evasion.
6. **Rivetbreaker (2), Mailed Dancer (5), Shieldwall (5), Bulwark Vow (4), Rampart Windfall (3), Long Campaign (5), 87 points.** Shieldwall's "30% less evasion" is free because Plated Hide already converts evasion to armour. I liked noticing that interaction. Bulwark Vow and Rampart Windfall are shield defence (185% total defences from shield). Long Campaign is for totem duration (50% skill effect duration with totems).
7. **Last 13 points:** Marching Drumbeat (5, "aura" as the warcry stand-in), Tower Shield Rank (4), Banner Advance (4). These are filler. I picked them because nothing else matched. I cannot tell what would count as a warcry-related stat, so I am not sure Drumbeat or Banner Advance help.

## 3. The build I ended with

- **Points:** 100/100. 15 notables, 2 keystones (Plated Hide, Shieldwall), 48 small, 35 roads.
- **Notables:** Roadside Effigy, Quickraised Pennant, Warstandard Muster, Brigandine Stride, Gambeson Mantle, Veteran's Sinew, Campfire Resolve, Rivetbreaker, Mailed Dancer, Bulwark Vow, Rampart Windfall, Long Campaign, Marching Drumbeat, Tower Shield Rank, Banner Advance.
- **Totals from `summary`:** 248% armour, 84% evasion (converted), 58% max life, 3% life regen per second, +22% all elemental resistances, +10% max block and +12% block with a shield, 185% defences from shield, 106% totem damage, 66% totem cast speed, 30% totem attack speed, 50% totem duration, +210 strength, +160 dexterity.
- **Score: 6/10.**
  - Defence side: 9/10. Life and armour are plentiful, I have a clear keystone synergy, and the roads deliver value.
  - Totem side: 6/10. I got about 106% totem damage and speed, and no extra totem, no totem life, no totem area or placement nodes, so the totems are single and fragile.
  - Warcry side: 0/10. There is no warcry content in the tree that I could find (high confidence for my `find` terms, medium that I did not miss a different wording). The build is "totem plus tank", with a few fillers for the shout half.
  - About 15 points are filler (attack-with-shield and Fortified damage for an auto-attack build, which does little for a totem user).

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree for the defence half, because the roads give real value and a few keystones combine well. The two biggest problems are that a Reaver is sealed into one arm plus the hub, so the str arm's life and the Idle Hands totem keystone are unreachable without warning, and that warcries do not exist at all. Totem support is thin. The two best things are the roads that double as defence, and the Plated Hide plus Shieldwall pairing. Notable-per-point value stayed high, since my path never felt padded.
