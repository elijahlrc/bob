# Playtest 12: Shade (dex/int) as a life-based melee tank

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/12.json`. 100 of 100 points spent. Tool calls used: about 30.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big star. Six regions radiate from a small central hub that holds the class starts. Each region is a mix of lane-like clusters (the Shade corner is a very flat, long "shelf" along the top right) and hexagon-ish grids (int, str and strdex have regular hex meshes near the centre).
- Labels make the notable names easy to scan, and the keystone discs are easy to spot. In the picture the Strength region (lower left) looks close: the Vanguard start sits only about 1,000 units from the Shade start, and "Strongarm" and "Blood Rite" are visible near the middle. My plan, as a PoE player, was to walk out of the Shade corner through the hub and into the Strength region, the way you do from the Scion area.
- The central hub is dense with life and attribute nodes. The names are charming but the picture alone does not say what they give.
- The Shade corner reads as an "attack/trap/mine/dagger/poison/crit" playground. The names (Powder and Wire, Wide-Mouthed Snare, Hungering Stiletto) make it obvious that this is not a melee tank area. Nothing in the picture told me the Strength region would be closed to me.
- Looks pointless: the many trap and mine notables stacked in the first ring (nine or more within about 8 points). A dozen or so "+30 to X" notables also read as filler.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Plan:** life, regeneration, armour, 1H melee damage. Went to the str region first (Bloodwell, Marrow Reservoir, Mossgrave Rest, Stonehewn Brawn, Monolith Skin, Lone Hammer are all there and are exactly what I wanted).
2. **`alloc 1618` (Bloodwell) said "cannot be reached"**, and so did every other Strength node, the Vanguard start and all Dex region nodes. I tried `reach 30` and `reach 80`: only hub and dexint nodes get a cost. The "-" cost on str, strint, int, dex and strdex notables, even when they are 3 to 5 steps from my build (Mirror Folio, Tall Pine Poise, Quickened Heartbeat), means the other class starts act as walls. This is the biggest finding: a Shade cannot leave dexint plus the hub. There was no message explaining why. I only worked it out from `show 4` (the Vanguard start is adjacent to hub road #77, which I could reach, yet the start itself has cost "-").
3. **Re-plan:** take the hub's life ring and the few life and armour things in dexint.
4. **`alloc 44` Pilgrim's Rest, 9 points.** The road passes Strength, Damage, Intelligence, Strength, Maximum Life, Dexterity, Life, Life and then the notable. The result is +25% maximum life for 9 points. Good value, the best deal on the tree for me.
5. **`alloc 47 48`, 2 points.** Two more 5% life smalls beside it, so life is now 35%.
6. **`alloc 88` Shared Sorrow, 9 points.** The road is Strength, ES, Strength, Strength, Damage, Intelligence, Armour/Evasion, Armour/Evasion, then the notable. The result is only +32% armour (and the same evasion) for 9 points, because 6 of the 9 nodes are irrelevant. Poor.
7. **`alloc 54` Compass of Marrow, 6 points.** Brought 3 Damage smalls and a Strength road. Fine: 7% x 3 plus 16% damage and attack speed.
8. **`alloc 93` Crossroads Bones, 4 points; `alloc 61` Wanderer's Tithe, 9 points (+8% all ele res, 5% life, but the way there is ES, Strength, Dexterity, Life, Strength, Intelligence and two resistance smalls); `alloc 776` Alley Sinew, 4 points (+30 Strength).**
9. At 43 points the hub was done: what remains is Common Grave (0.4% regen with mana regen, 9 points), Hollow Heart (ES) and the Strongarm and Crimson Pact keystones (both of which would hurt: Crimson Pact removes life regeneration).
10. **`alloc 845` Taintedsteel Pace, 11 points, `alloc 855` Bolted Door, 11 points, `alloc 828` Knuckleduster Luck, 7 points.** `reach` had quoted 11, 14 and 18 for these, so I had expected to share more of the paths. They do not share. Each notable sits at the end of its own 7 to 10 node corridor of crit, ES, dexterity, intelligence and DoT multiplier smalls. Taintedsteel Pace in particular took 10 junk nodes (crit chance, ES, DoT multiplier) for 20% physical, 4% attack speed and an unwanted 4% extra chaos. After this I had 28 points left and nothing to show for 29 points but a few shield and physical numbers.
11. **After the third alloc the costs of nearby notables had dropped a lot** (Cutthroat's Spoils quoted 26 at the start, 10 now), because the corridors overlap with the ones already paid for. That made the later picks cheaper, but I had no way to know that early.
12. **`alloc 571` Lingering Rue, 7 points** (1% life regen). Path was ES, Int, Crit, Dex, DoT, DoT. **`alloc 831` Spoils of the Alley, 4 points** (6% life, +10 life per kill, and a 5% life small on the way). **`alloc 794` Cutthroat's Spoils, 10 points** (10% life, +20 Strength). The road there was nine nodes of Dexterity, Evasion, Intelligence, Damage, with one Maximum Life small. **`alloc 699` Gossamer Dusk, 3 points** (5% life, 0.5% regen). **`alloc 808` Fleet Shadow, 4 points** (9% attack speed and 5% movement speed).
13. Did not take Lone Hand Knack (the only dexint one-hand-melee notable): it was 14 points away at the end and 24 at the start. It is on the far (top right) edge of the shelf, behind everything else.

## 3. The build I ended with

- 100 / 100 points: 14 notables, 0 keystones, 53 small, 33 road/attribute nodes. 39 points in the hub and 61 in dexint.
- Notables: Pilgrim's Rest, Shared Sorrow, Compass of Marrow, Crossroads Bones, Wanderer's Tithe, Alley Sinew, Taintedsteel Pace, Bolted Door, Knuckleduster Luck, Lingering Rue, Spoils of the Alley, Cutthroat's Spoils, Gossamer Dusk, Fleet Shadow.
- Totals (from `summary`): +84% maximum life (plus 24 flat), 1.5% life regen per second, +20 life per kill (10 + 5 + 5 from three nodes), 32% armour, 143% evasion, 120% shield defences, +8% attack block, +24% all elemental resistances, 65% damage, 50% physical, 20% attack speed, +190 Strength, +140 Dex, +90 Int, 140% crit chance, 50% ES (unused), 20% chaos damage, 9% cast speed (unused), 5% movement speed, and a few more small bits.
- **Score 5 / 10.** Life is good (84%, which would have been about 100% in the Strength region), regen is thin (1.5%, where the Strength region has single notables giving 1.8%), and armour is poor: 32% increased, with no flat armour, no armour notable of any weight and no physical damage reduction. Melee damage is weak: no melee, one-hand or weapon notable beyond a +20% physical, a +20% physical from Knuckleduster Luck and the generic damage smalls. Meanwhile I carry about 25 points' worth of nodes that do nothing for the brief (crit chance 140%, ES 50%, cast speed, chaos, evasion 143%, mana, Intelligence). It works as a "fat life pool plus shield" character but it is far from the tank I wanted, and almost all of the gap comes from the wall around the Shade corner.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree only inside my corner. The two biggest problems: (1) other class starts wall the Shade into dexint plus the hub, so a build against the grain is impossible (and the tool never says so clearly); (2) corridors are filled with off-theme smalls, so notables cost 7 to 11 points of which most are useless to the build. The two best things: the hub life ring (Pilgrim's Rest, 25% life for 9 points) and the fact that overlapping corridors make later notables cheap.
