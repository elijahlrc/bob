# Playtest 02: Vanguard (strength) going Minion-Master

Build file: `scratchpad/audit/02.json`. Result in one line: **the minion nodes could not be reached at all.** A Vanguard can only buy nodes in the hub ring and the Vanguard (str) region. All 16 minion notables sit in the Zealot (strint) and Mystic (int) regions, behind other classes' start nodes. The tool refused every route there.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-armed star, with a small hub ring in the centre and the six class starts around it. Regions are fat petals. Each petal is a big mass of clusters, with one long tendril ending in a keystone (Living Ward, Searing Avatar, Volatile Servants and so on).
- Finding my way by picture is easy. The labels are readable and each region has its own character: the int region has hexagon-lattice cells, the str region is long diagonal roads with small clusters hanging off them. Colour coding works: the minion green-teal is spotted quickly in the strint region (Summoned Choir, Hurried Throng, Reliquary Mending and others around x -1000..-1700, y -1300..-2300).
- My region picture (str, lower left) is big and busy. Many notables are weapon-specific (Sword, Axe, Mace, Staff names) and look pointless to a non-attacker. Life and armour clusters are dense and obvious.
- First thought on seeing the strint picture: "the minion cluster is right next to my start, on the Zealot side of the hub, so I should be able to walk there." That is the plan the tool then broke.
- On the whole picture I could see no line crossing from one petal to the next, only the hub ring tying them together. I did not know whether that was deliberate.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Wanted:** minion damage, life and speed. `find minion` returned 16 notables. Zero are in str or hub. Fifteen are in strint and int, one is in dexint (Understudy's Guard, block for minions). The best-looking cluster is strint: Sacristan's Pact, Reliquary Mending, Ossuary Mender, Processional Haste, Hurried Throng, Summoned Choir and others.
2. **Plan:** `reach 40` listed Sacristan's Pact under the "cost 21" band, Sanctified Fervor under 26, Reliquary Mending under 28, and so on. I planned a spend of about 21 points for the first one, then about 40 points to collect the cluster.
3. **Failure:** `alloc 1899` returned "cannot be reached". So did 1865, 2084, 1902, 1880, 1875, 2142, 262, 1479, 785, 1484, 1155, and the class starts 5 and 3. Fourteen refusals in a row, in three regions (strint, int, strdex, dexint, dex).
4. **Spent 11 points** to reach the hub road #87 next to the Zealot start, hoping that opening the road would open the way into strint. It did not. `show 5` (Zealot start) has only two links: #87 (hub) and #2084 (strint). `alloc 2084` was refused even with #87 taken. Class starts are the only door between the hub and a region, and other classes' starts are closed.
5. **Accidentally took Cairnlight Well** (8 points) while probing. `near 1832` showed it is a dead end at the edge of str and does not touch strint. I later retook it on purpose (it has mana and cheaper skills).
6. **Systematic check:** `reach 60` printed a numeric cost only for hub and str nodes. Every other node (about 340 notables in five regions) shows `cost -`. 60 hops covers the whole tree, so this is not a distance limit.
7. **Fallback plan** (a real minion player who can't get minion nodes): get tough, keep mana for summon costs, and pick up strength-based life and armour. I skipped everything weapon-specific.
8. **Life cluster** (about 27 points): Bloodwell, Marrow Reservoir, Stonehewn Brawn, Forgeproof Blood, Tombhearth Blood, Ossuary Heart. The roads to these are long chains of small Life nodes and Strength roads, so the actual spend was 3 to 5 points each. Good value.
9. **Armour/resist:** Monolith Skin (2), Roadworn Plate (5, +12% all resistances), Tankard Mail (4), Mossgrave Rest (2, regeneration).
10. **Hub:** Hollow Heart (3, used the spur I had already paid for), Crossroads Bones (7, +12 all attributes, hopefully helps a minion gem's int requirement), Common Grave (6, mana regeneration).
11. **Mana:** Cairnlight Well (2).
12. **Keystone decisions:**
    - Blood Rite (7): Skills cost life, and summoning costs mana which a strength character has little of. Took it.
    - Idle Hands (24): "You deal no damage with your own skills" reads like the keystone for a minion player, and the chain was cheap (the line is mostly Life Regeneration and Strength smalls). But its upside is only extra totems and brands, not minions, and the cluster next to it is brand duration. It is a trap-in-waiting: I took it because the theme says "minions fight for me", and with zero minion nodes it does not help any minion. See friction #6.
13. **Refunds:** `unalloc` on a spur gives back only the nodes that nothing depends on. Several calls printed "not taken, or others depend on it". It was workable but fiddly.

## 3. The build I ended with

- **Points used:** 100 of 100.
- **Totals:** 14 notables, 2 keystones (Blood Rite, Idle Hands), 48 small, 36 road/attribute. Regions: hub 23, str 77.
- **Notables:** Bloodwell, Marrow Reservoir, Stonehewn Brawn, Forgeproof Blood, Tombhearth Blood, Ossuary Heart, Monolith Skin, Hollow Heart, Crossroads Bones, Cairnlight Well, Mossgrave Rest, Roadworn Plate, Tankard Mail, Common Grave.
- **Summary totals (before Common Grave):** +390 Strength, 158% increased max Life, +56 max Life, 170% increased Armour, +90 Armour, +36% all elemental resistances, 40% increased max Mana, 11% reduced mana cost, regenerate 4.2% Life per second, +24 all attributes, 27% increased max Energy Shield, 3% movement speed, 20% flask life recovery.
- **Minion nodes:** zero. No minion life, damage or speed anywhere.
- **Score for the brief: 2 / 10.** It is a sturdy, tanky Vanguard with a life pool and resistances, so the "some survivability for yourself" half of the brief is well served. But the three things the brief put first (minion damage, life and speed) are absent, and nothing in the build supports minions except mana and attributes. A Vanguard cannot play this character on this tree.

## 5. Overall

I would not enjoy planning on this tree if I wanted anything other than my own class's petal: the structure walls every class into its own region plus a small hub. For a Vanguard it is a nice life-and-armour sandbox, but the brief (minions) was impossible.

Biggest problems:

1. Foreign class starts block all travel, so 15 of 16 minion notables are out of reach for a Vanguard.
2. `reach` and `alloc` do not explain why: unreachable nodes sit in cost bands with no warning.

Best things:

1. Life and armour clusters in str are generous and clear.
2. Colours and region pictures make searching fast.
