# Playtest 10: Zealot who wants a bow

Build file: `.../audit/10.json`. Class: Zealot (strength + intelligence). Goal: a bow character (projectiles, attack speed, accuracy, some life). Budget 100 points.

**Headline finding:** the tool will not let a Zealot touch a single bow, dex, dex/int, str/dex, str or int node. Every other region is sealed behind its own class start, and a class start cannot be taken. A Zealot can only ever use the strint region and the hub. The brief ("cross the tree") has no solution on this tree. All 331 non-strint, non-hub notables that `reach` lists for a Zealot are untakeable, and `reach` does not say so.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-armed wheel. The hub is in the middle, six class starts sit around it, and each class has a large arm. Strint (mine) is the upper-left arm, with a long road running up and to the left. I assumed I could walk through the hub and out into any arm. The picture does nothing to say otherwise.
- The class-start discs are drawn as plain white dots on the hub ring. They look like waypoints you pass through. They read exactly like ordinary nodes sitting on the road to the other arms. Nothing says "wall".
- Strint reads as mana, minions, staves, fire and spells. The labels in the region picture say so: Mana Bastion, Searing Avatar, Sigil Warden, Living Ward, Prismatic Balance, Blood Rite, "Staff" notables, "Choir" and "Throng" for minions. The dex arm picture is full of bow names (Steady Draw, Close Quarters, Ridge Archer's Pace, Rapid Nockfall), so it is easy to see where my build "lives": lower right, about 1,500 to 2,500 units from my start at (-693,-400).
- Looks pointless: the six thin radial lines (the spokes) and the hex-cell patches in the int and str arms are just decoration or cluster frames. In the dex-region picture the labels pile on top of each other (Hemlock Draught, Venomed Fletching, Hushed Weathervane, Thornneedle Dance, Whistling Shaft, Tailwind Shaft, Shaftpiercer Hunt). I could read the bow notables only after using `region dex`. The pictures are good for orientation, bad for picking nodes.
- Easy to find the way, as long as the wheel is walkable. Strongarm sits right next to the centre. That looked like a "this class can shoot arrows" hint, and in a way it is.

## 2. Allocation log

1. `new zealot --budget 100`. `legend` read fine.
2. `reach 8`. Only strint notables show up (Wayfarer's Grace +30 Dex at cost 3, Pealing Bells, Chalice Deep and more mana). Nothing bow-like. So I searched: `find bow` gives 42 nodes, all in dex, dexint and strdex, none in strint or hub. `find projectile` gives one hub keystone, **Strongarm** ("Strength's Damage bonus applies to Projectile Attacks"), plus dex notables.
3. `region hub` and `region dex`: listed 72 dex notables. The list is long and ordered by distance, but the distance is not real (see step 4), so it did not help me choose.
4. Decision 1: take Strongarm. It cost 9 and passed through 4 road nodes (+30 Str, +10 Dex, +8 all attributes) and 4 small nodes (7% damage, 8% mana). That was the only "bow-friendly" thing on offer for a Zealot, and a good one: strength becomes projectile damage. I would have built around it if a bow had been reachable. At that point I was happy.
5. Decision 2: try to cross. `alloc 886` (Ridge Archer's Pace) gives "cannot be reached". So do 887, 1218 (Tall Pine Poise), 2 (Strider start), 1126 (the first dex node next to the Strider start), 1287 (strdex), 1512 (strdex), 1845 (str), 456 (int), 831 (dexint), 1618, 1650. All "cannot be reached". `reach 100` shows 401 notables, of which 331 are untakeable and only 70 (strint + hub) can be taken.
6. Why: `show 2` and `show 60` show the hub road #60 linking only to the Strider start #2, and #2 linking to #1126 on the dex side. The only door into the dex arm is the Strider start. Same for the other arms (`near 3` shows the same thing for the Reaver start). I found no other link between arms. I assumed a closed start is meant to be impassable, but then the layout has one door per arm and the player has no way round. **Medium confidence on "intended": the tool gives no message. High confidence that the layout leaves no detour.**
7. Decision 3: tried Compass of Marrow (hub, +16% damage, +4% attack speed), hoping the hub's east end would lead on. It cost 18 (a long chain of strength/damage roads from the centre). It led nowhere. Tall Pine Poise showed "cost 9" in `reach` but cannot be taken. I should have looked at `show` first. Wasted: about 18 points for one notable I could have had for much less (see frictions).
8. Decision 4: make a "bow-shaped" Zealot out of strint and hub only.
   - Wayfarer's Grace (+30 Dex, 3 points). Pilgrim's Stride (accuracy, attack speed, +20 Dex, 9). Chorister's Eye (+30% accuracy, 2 on top of Sanctified Fervor's road). Sanctified Fervor (+14% damage, +5% attack speed, 7). Pilgrim's Rest (+10% life, 3). Crossroads Bones (4). Marrow Hymn (8% life, +20 Str, 6). Hollow Heart (3). Brimstone Hymn (12% phys, 12% elemental, 5% phys as extra fire, 10). Smoldering Heresy (18% elemental, 5% penetration, 3). Tallow Heart (+30 life, 10% life, 11). Flagellant's Spark (24% elemental damage with attacks, 3% pen, 10).
   - Many of these notables are cast-speed, minion or spell ones. I ignored those lines.
9. Final: 98 of 100 points, 14 notables or keystones. I stopped because the next useful notables cost more than I had left.

## 3. The build I ended with

98/100 points. Notables: Strongarm (keystone), Compass of Marrow, Wayfarer's Grace, Pilgrim's Stride, Pilgrim's Rest, Crossroads Bones, Sanctified Fervor, Chorister's Eye, Marrow Hymn, Hollow Heart, Brimstone Hymn, Smoldering Heresy, Tallow Heart, Flagellant's Spark. 37 road nodes, 47 small nodes.

Totals from `summary` (the ones that matter for a bow): +290 Str, +80 Dex, +120 Int, 95% increased max life (+30), 72% increased damage, 42% increased physical damage, 40% increased elemental damage (24% of it "with Attacks"), 14% increased attack speed, 60% increased accuracy rating, 5% + 3% elemental penetration, 5% phys as extra fire, +4% movement speed, Strongarm. Dead weight: 80% mana, 42% ES, 10% cast speed, 25% minion damage, 15% phys-to-fire "with spells", "effect of non-damaging ailments", "effect of auras".

**Score: 4/10.** It is a playable Zealot attacker: life, damage, attack speed and accuracy are all there, and Strongarm turns Zealot strength into projectile damage, which is a real build hook. But it has **no bow damage, no projectile speed or projectile damage, no bow crit and no bow-specific notable at all**. A character built this way is "a Zealot who holds a bow", not a bow character. About 30 of my 98 points are attribute roads and small mana/ES nodes I did not want.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning builds on this tree only as a Zealot who stays in his arm. The two biggest problems: a class cannot leave its arm at all (the start discs are walls, and the tools and pictures hide it), and `reach` lists 331 nodes I cannot take, with costs that look real. The two best things: Strongarm (a clear, well-placed hook that turns strength into archery), and the readable `alloc`/`summary` output, which made choices easy to check. Strint itself is rich in life, mana and spell nodes, but nothing in it serves an archer beyond generic damage and accuracy.
