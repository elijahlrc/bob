# Playtest 03: Strider, bow / crit / evasion

Build file: `C:/Users/littl/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/E--Proj-Auto/cfb7adfb-b8bd-4736-8b0b-db97344ecab5/scratchpad/audit/03.json`. Tool calls used: about 20.

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-armed star. Six class starts sit in a small ring in the middle. Each region is a cluster of cluster-islands joined by thin grey roads. Hexagon honeycombs fill the int, str and strdex regions. The dex region (lower right, mine) has no honeycomb. It is a loose scatter of small ring-clusters with long thin roads between them.
- Only keystones and a few class starts carry labels in the whole-tree picture. I could not tell where any notable was, or what it did, without the region picture. The whole picture is a map of shapes only, so it does not help me plan.
- The region picture is the useful one. Names are readable, but there are many overlaps (for example "Frostfletch Gale" / "Whistling Shaft" / "Archer's Pace", and "Hemlock Draught" / "Thornneedle Dance" / "Venomed Fletching"). Some labels cannot be read at all. The ids are the only reliable handle.
- Names are evocative (Hawk's Zenith, Lone Hawk's Cry, Rapid Nockfall, Steady Draw). Colour does help: orange is attack, green is evasion. I could guess "the orange cluster near the top middle is the bow stuff" and I was right.
- The Strider start sits at the left edge of the region picture. The bow notables are 12 or more steps out to the right, and the evasion notables are 11 or more steps south. The start has no notable next to it.
- The dex region looks big (72 notables and keystones). A large share of it is obviously not for me: sword, dagger, claw, dual wield, shield, trap, totem, mine, flask and mana notables fill the right half and the south. The brief's whole build is in the left 60%.
- Looks pointless: the long flask / mana / life-recovery strip at the top right (Willowbark Tonic, Poultice Moss and others, 33 to 41 points out). Also the far-right column, which is so far away that nobody will reach it with 100 points.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Plan.** I wanted bow damage, crit chance and multiplier, attack and projectile speed, evasion, and a keystone to survive. I ran `region dex` and `find Bow`. The bow notables are one big neighbourhood (Trailwarden's Draw 887, Lone Hawk's Cry 895, Hawk's Zenith 905, Shaftpiercer Hunt 894, Rapid Nockfall 900, Ridge Archer's Pace 886) with "Damage with Bows" small nodes between them. That is good and easy to read.
2. **Trailwarden's Draw (887), cost 12.** The route was Accuracy, Dex road, Dex road, Life, Dex, Accuracy, Dex, Life, Dex, Bow dmg, Bow dmg, notable. Nine of the 12 nodes are generic filler: accuracy, +10 Dex, 5% life. Only the last two small nodes are bow nodes. Cost 12 for the first notable was a surprise, since it looked closer on the picture. I note this as friction #1.
3. **Lone Hawk's Cry (895) cost 5 and Hawk's Zenith (905) cost 5.** Both were cheap because they branch off the 887 area. Each route again had one accuracy small, one Dex small and two bow-damage or bow-crit smalls. This was the best stretch: 22 points for 3 bow notables, with +130% crit chance and +30% multiplier. 22/100.
4. **Shaftpiercer Hunt (894) 2, Rapid Nockfall (900) 3, Ridge Archer's Pace (886) 3.** These are the attack speed and damage notables. 30/100. Reach listed them clearly with their cost after each step, which helped a lot.
5. **Crit: Featherblade Omen (1038) 6, Falcon's Descent (1123) 2 more, Tailwind Shaft (915) 4.** 42/100. Falcon's Descent was cheap because it follows Featherblade. +52% total crit multiplier from these.
6. **Eagle's Windmark (1202) 6, Whistling Shaft (912) 2.** Evasion and accuracy, then projectile speed. 50/100.
7. **Hawk's Wardwood (1212) 2, Cold Clarity (1228) 3, Dewpoint Aim (921) 4, Hawkflight Quarrel (916) 3.** 62/100. Hawk's Wardwood is cheap, but it is accuracy again. I was already past 100% accuracy at this point, so I should not have taken Dewpoint Aim and Wardwood. I did it because there was nothing better at that price. I note this as friction #4.
8. **Skipped on purpose:**
   - Prismfeather Shot (933), Stormfeather Barb (926), Rimebite Barb (1120), Frostfletch Gale (927) and Hoarthorn Frost (930) are elemental and cold damage with attacks. A physical bow wants these only if it converts, and the only conversion is 20% (Hoarthorn). I could not see what a converted build would look like, so I left the whole group out.
   - Close Quarters (1150), Steady Draw (1155). Steady Draw is 25% more damage for 20% less attack speed, which is exactly neutral (1.25 x 0.8 = 1.0). Close Quarters has no numbers on it, and it sounds bad for a kiting evasion bow.
9. **Wildwood Skirmisher (954) 8, Fleetwit Hunter (983) 2 more.** 72/100. Fleetwit has crit chance, damage and move speed. A good hybrid notable. The route to Wildwood had two life smalls and two dex smalls.
10. **Evasion pack: Stalker's Hide (957) 5, Mistcloak Drift (962) 4, Keenhand Quiver (974) 5, Thistledown Step (984) 4.** 90/100. Each is 4 to 5 steps off a shared hub. Evasion notables are 20% to 40% each. I was getting +270% evasion for about 25 points. Keenhand was +8% attack speed, which is worth the price.
11. **Gale-Footed Stalk (975) 2, Fleetblood Hunt (1192) 3, Leafshade Slip (971) 5.** 100/100 and a complete build, with no keystone.
12. **Reconsidered.** After `show 1165` I noticed Nimble Gambit (30% dodge) was cheap (cost 5 with the Leafshade road in place). I wanted it more than Leafshade (+40% evasion). `unalloc 971` gave back only 1 point (the notable). Four stub nodes of the road stayed and I had to find them and refund them with `unalloc 969 970 944 952`. After that Nimble Gambit cost 7, not 5. I refunded Fleetblood Hunt (3) and took Nimble Gambit through four "Movement Speed" smalls (952, 944, 1161 to 1164). Final 99/100.

## 3. The build I ended with

- **Points used:** 99 of 100, all in the dex region. 22 notables, 1 keystone (Nimble Gambit), 57 small, 19 road or attribute nodes.
- **Notables:** Trailwarden's Draw, Lone Hawk's Cry, Hawk's Zenith, Shaftpiercer Hunt, Rapid Nockfall, Ridge Archer's Pace, Featherblade Omen, Falcon's Descent, Tailwind Shaft, Eagle's Windmark, Whistling Shaft, Hawk's Wardwood, Cold Clarity, Dewpoint Aim, Hawkflight Quarrel, Wildwood Skirmisher, Fleetwit Hunter, Stalker's Hide, Mistcloak Drift, Keenhand Quiver, Thistledown Step, Gale-Footed Stalk.
- **Totals (from `summary`):**
  - Damage: 192% increased Damage with Bows (and the same for Damage over Time with a Bow), 43% with Projectiles, 40% with Attacks with Projectiles, 20% with Attacks.
  - Speed: 33% Attack Speed with Bows + 35% Attack Speed, 93% Projectile Speed, 43% Movement Speed.
  - Crit: 162% + 95% crit chance, +54% + 72% crit multiplier.
  - Defence: 270% increased Evasion + 210 flat, 30% Dodge, 53% increased maximum Life + 22 flat, +14% all elemental resistances.
  - Other: 197% increased Accuracy, +310 Dex.
- **Score: 8 / 10.** All three words of the brief are served (bow speed and damage, crit, evasion) and the tree let me find and buy each. Marks off: the character has no way to stand a hit. It has +14% resistances, no armour, no stun avoidance, no chaos resistance, no leech or on-hit recovery (those are in other regions), and life is only 53% increased. The crit multiplier is moderate (+126% in total). No keystone changes how the build plays, since Nimble Gambit is only a defence choice. About 20 points went into accuracy that I did not need.

## 5. Overall

I would enjoy planning on this tree. The bow neighbourhood is dense and clear, and `reach` with costs made planning easy.

Two biggest problems: (1) the first 12 points are mostly accuracy and +10 Dex filler before any notable; (2) there is too much accuracy everywhere, and not enough recovery, resistances or a reason to pick a keystone (Steady Draw is neutral, Close Quarters has no numbers). A refund also leaves dead stub nodes.

Two best things: the six bow notables sit together with bow smalls in between; and the keystone road (Nimble Gambit via movement speed) teaches the build.
