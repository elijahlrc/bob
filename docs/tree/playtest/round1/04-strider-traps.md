# Playtest 04: Strider who wants to be a trap and mine thrower

Build file: `C:/Users/littl/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/E--Proj-Auto/cfb7adfb-b8bd-4736-8b0b-db97344ecab5/scratchpad/audit/04.json` (100/100 points, strider).

## 1. First impression from the pictures

- The whole tree is a big six-wedge snowflake around a small hub. Each class start sits just outside the hub; each wedge fans out far from it. The wedges look like separate lands. The faint lines between wedges look like borders.
- The Strider wedge (lower right) reads as an archer land. The labels I can read in `region-dex.png` are Bows, Projectile Speed, Evasion, Accuracy, Daggers, Swords, Claws, Poison, Flasks, Dual Wielding. I saw no trap or mine words anywhere near the start.
- The traps and mines are in the Shade wedge (upper right), in `region-dexint.png`. Gaping Pitfall, Wide-Mouthed Snare, Warren of Snares, Wicked Tripwire, Sleeper Cache and Patient Tripline form a dense, obviously themed block just above the Shade start, a few hundred units from the hub. The block looks like it is next door to the Strider start. Moss-Eyed Idol, a Strider notable, sits almost touching Sleeper Cache across the dex/dexint line.
- I expected a short walk from the Strider start across that line into the trap block, like crossing a border in a PoE tree. Whether the border can be crossed is the biggest question of this playtest (see below).
- It is easy to get a rough sense of where things are from the colours and the six labelled starts. It is hard to read a single node: names overlap badly in the zoomed regions (for example "Thornneedle Dance / Venomed Fletching / Hemlock Draught", "Fervid Thicket / Heartwood Sinew"). Many labels cannot be read.
- Several long, thin, empty-looking roads of grey dots stretch from the middle to the far edges. The far right of the Strider wedge is full of dots that look pointless until you know that Thornsnare Warren is out there.

## 2. Allocation log

1. **Tool start.** `new strider --budget 100` gave start node #2. `find trap` showed 14 trap notables: 1 in dex (Thornsnare Warren, cost 36), 10 in dexint, some brand/totem ones elsewhere. The dexint ones had cost "-". I took that to mean "too far". It did not.
2. **Trying to reach the trap block.** I wanted Warren of Snares, Wicked Tripwire, Wide-Mouthed Snare and the rest of the dexint block. `alloc 537` (Sleeper Cache) answered `cannot be reached`. I tried 11 more dexint nodes (small nodes, notables, even #1 the Shade start): every one said `cannot be reached`. I tried one node of every other region too: same.
   - `show 1` (shade start) shows its links: a road in the hub (#53, cost 11 for me) and one small node in dexint (#726). `alloc 53` works (11 points). `alloc 726` and `alloc 1` do not. So the class start acts as a wall: you can stand next to it but never pass through.
   - `near 1187` (Moss-Eyed Idol) shows it is a dead end on the dex side. No link crosses the dex/dexint line.
   - Result: as a Strider I could take nodes in the dex wedge and the hub only. Everything that I thought of as my archetype (about 12 notables, 2 of them with "+2 traps and mines") was unreachable. Cost: about 15 tool calls before I understood the rule.
3. **What dex does have for traps and mines.** Only 2 notables: Thornsnare Warren (30% trap damage, 20% trap area, +2 traps; cost 36) and Buried Thornseed (18% mine area; cost 21). Both lie on the road, with trap/mine small nodes (16% trap damage, 14% mine damage, area, cast speed) just before them.
4. **I took Thornsnare Warren first (36).** The path is 35 nodes of road: Dexterity, Accuracy, Life, Evasion, Attack Speed (+10 Dex, +10% accuracy and so on) down a very long arm to the far right. I did not choose any of those nodes, they came with the road. By the time I got to the trap notable I had spent over a third of the build on accuracy and evasion (see the table). I allocated it by mistake first when testing "cannot be reached", then refunded the end nodes only: `unalloc` gave back the notable and not the road (37 points stayed in the build). I restarted with `new`.
5. **Crit.** Featherblade Omen (45% crit chance, +12% multi) and Falcon's Descent (+40% multi) hang off the same road: 2 points each. Good value, and generic (not weapon-specific), so they work for traps.
6. **Cold Clarity (3), Quickened Heartbeat (3).** Crit chance and multi (via small nodes) and cast speed; traps use cast speed in this game.
7. **Buried Thornseed (14).** Another 14 points via the south road, 2 small mine nodes (14% mine damage and 6% area each) on the way.
8. **Fleetwit Hunter (4), Quarry's Tithe (2, mana), Compass of Marrow (9, in the hub: 16% damage).** Fleetwit's 20% crit chance is generic. Its 16% damage is "with Attacks" and is wasted for me.
9. **Last 24 points (survival and filler).** Hushed Weathervane (2, 8% less mana reserved: mines reserve mana, but the other half of the line is auras), Sapwood Vigor (3, 15% life), Thornheart Resolve (4), Wanderer's Tithe (6, resists), Crossroads Bones (7), Hawk's Wardwood (2). I could not find anything more on-theme within 9 points. Almost everything in reach is bow, projectile, dagger, sword, claw, poison, flask or evasion. None of it affects a trap.

## 3. The build I ended with

- **Points:** 100/100. 15 notables, 0 keystones, 54 small, 31 road/attribute (78 in dex, 22 in the hub).
- **Notables:** Thornsnare Warren, Featherblade Omen, Falcon's Descent, Cold Clarity, Quickened Heartbeat, Buried Thornseed, Fleetwit Hunter, Quarry's Tithe, Compass of Marrow, Hushed Weathervane, Sapwood Vigor, Thornheart Resolve, Wanderer's Tithe, Crossroads Bones, Hawk's Wardwood.
- **Totals that matter for a trap/mine thrower (from `summary`):**
  - Traps: +62% damage, +20% area, +8% cast speed with traps, +2 traps.
  - Mines: +28% damage, +30% area, no extra mines.
  - Crit: +95% crit chance, +72% crit multiplier, 6% cast speed, +37% damage.
  - Other: 86% life, 38% resists, 23% mana.
- **Waste:** over 100% increased accuracy rating, +280 dexterity, 48% evasion, 13% attack speed, 20% attack damage. None of these helps a trap (traps do not use accuracy or attack speed, and I do not need evasion). About 35 of my 100 points went on these "free" road nodes.
- **Brief score: 4 out of 10.** The crit is good and I do have traps, plus a little area. But:
  - Compare to the Shade block that I could not reach: Wicked Tripwire alone is 50% crit chance and +30% multiplier for traps and mines, for one notable. Warren of Snares in dexint gives 30%/30% damage and +2 traps and +2 mines.
  - I got half the trap damage and none of the extra mines.
  - The only way to the "real" archetype is to start as a Shade, so the answer to "what does the tree do with a Strider who walks toward an archetype elsewhere?" is "it forbids it", with no hint in the tool or the picture.

## 5. Overall

I would not enjoy planning this build. It was fun to find the crit nodes, but the tree does not let a Strider become anything but a Strider. Walling off foreign class starts is very strict, and nothing in the picture or the tool says so.

Two biggest problems: (1) the class starts are impassable, so a "go to another archetype" plan is impossible, yet the picture shows the trap block touching the Strider wedge; (2) the only trap notable in my wedge costs a 35-node road of accuracy and evasion.

Two best things: the generic crit notables 2 or 3 points off the road, and the tool's cost listing (`reach`).
