## What the two rounds say, together

Twenty-four builds, two-thirds of them against the grain of their start, reached 7 or 8 of 10 once the regions were joined. The
shape players liked: one dense district with themed small nodes leading to notables 2 to 5 points apart ("cost falls as the build
grows"), cheap `reach` pricing, roads whose nodes mostly served the way. What they disliked was almost always one of three
things: finding a theme, a road through nodes not for them, and a missing piece of a build (life on hit, poison spread, warcry,
stun power, curses near a Mystic). The first two are changed; the third needs new nodes.
