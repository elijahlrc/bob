## Specializations: their notables, partners and twins

A notable belongs to a family when a third or more of its cluster's units are that family (resistances, movement and flasks are generic and left out). Partner: the cluster's other families. Nearest twin: steps to the closest notable of the same family (CONSTRAINTS §5.2 wants about five or more). **Same pairing** marks two notables of the family whose partners are the same, which is not a choice (§5.1).

## Empty space

Cells of 100×100 tree units with no node within 170 units, by the region of the nearest node. **Inside** is within the network's own outline (by 5° sector, the farthest node there or beside it): room to fill without growing. **Rim** is between that outline and the circle through the farthest node (radius 4725): room to grow outward. A notable with three smalls in a tight cluster takes about four to six cells with the room round it.
