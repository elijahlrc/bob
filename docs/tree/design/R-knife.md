# The Knife's rebuild: plan (2026-10-30)

The first region of the attrition rework, on the approved skeleton (R-skeleton.md). The sketch is `R-knife.json` /
`R-knife.png`. Every cluster keeps its nodes and lines from today's network (the lines are repriced later, CONSTRAINTS.md
§16 item 2); what changes is where it sits and what it hangs from. Positions below are (radius, degrees off the Knife's
angle of -30; negative is the Spire's side). Steps are counted from the Shade's start along the roads.

## Roads

- **Start** at radius 1,450. **Exits:** north 5% energy shield, south 15% crit chance; then a hub of +15/+15 at step 2.
- **Bows to the seams:** from each hub, three road nodes out and back to a seam on the border (about step 5).
- **Spokes:** from each hub straight out, through a junction with the cross-link (step 4), the mid ring (step 9 or 10)
  and the rim ring (step 14 or 15).
- **Cross-link:** the two spokes' junctions joined through the home hub, Keen Instinct (step 6).
- **Mid ring and rim ring** across the region, **border radials** from each seam to the mid ring (the Knife builds both
  its seams and radials now; the Spire and the Bow link to them in their passes).

## Walks (20 points)

- **The Shade's caster (chaos spells):** north exit (energy shield), hub, the north spoke to the cross-link junction,
  Spoils (mana), Thick Blood (life), on up the spoke past Quiet Knife's side room (dagger spells, optional) to the mid
  ring and the Stair's wheel (Rotting Word). Gives up: the south half entirely, Keen Instinct unless it detours one
  step. Chose: mana and life before the first chaos node, at step 10.
- **The Shade's attacker (poison daggers):** south exit (crit), hub, Soft Step, the cross-link to Keen Instinct and
  Quick Wrists (attack speed), back to the south spoke, Fine Point at step 9, Smeared Edge at 11. Gives up: energy
  shield and the north's poison wheel (the Rookery), three steps away across the axis.
- **A Strider coming in:** from the Bow's seam (Grey Cloak) up the south border radial to Tumble and Nimble Gambit:
  dodge and evasion, no weapon.
- **Late (120 points):** the north spoke's keystones (Shade Leech, Cruel Agony) against the south's (Nimble Gambit),
  each a long detour from the other half; the rim ring carries a late build across.
