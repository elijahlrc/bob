# G4 fix brief, round 1

Written 2026-10-22 from the six reports of G4 audit round 1 (`audit/growth-1/`), for the region agents who carry out
the fixes and for the coordinator. [CONSTRAINTS.md](../CONSTRAINTS.md) wins over everything here; read it in full
before any edit. Nothing in `src/` or `scripts/` was changed to write this.

**Inputs.** The six reports (network, trade-offs, discipline, starts, looks, builds); G-growth §0 to §4; `G3/borders.md`
and the user's two decisions after it (about five **seam clusters**, one per seam, each a combination a build from either
side would want, bringing borders toward 8 to 10 links; **no Festering–Pitch Arrows link**); and the measures with the
corrected allocator, `G4/builds-start.txt` (now) against `G0/builds-base2.txt` (before the growth, same tool). Distances
and plan counts below were re-measured on the tree of 8caa171 with a scratch script (`g4-fix1/` in the session
scratchpad): "in N plans" means N of the 28 archetypes' best plans at 120 points.

**Already done, not in the work list:**

- **The six reference names are renamed** (8caa171): Thick Skin → Ox Hide, Executioner → Finishing Cut, Heavy Draw →
  Deep Pull, Arcane Focus → Ready Wit, Devotion → Kept Vows, Overcharge → Live Current. Ids are unchanged.
  `src/data/passives/names.test.ts` now checks every notable and keystone name against the reference tree. This brief uses
  the new names throughout; the audits use the old ones. (Discipline F1.)
- **The builds audit's tool items §3 to §5** (ba362b5): the allocator pays for its gear's attributes; `fn:*` matches
  untagged lines; the archetypes weigh stun defences, ES recovery, grit, insight, tagged trap/mine/brand crit,
  `doubleDamage`, `cullingStrike` and the on-full-life lines; 15 more keystones are weighed.

**How to read a work item.** Each row names the finding it answers (report and number: N = network, T = trade-offs,
D = discipline, S = starts, L = looks, B = builds), the exact nodes, the change, and what it must not break. Lines are
written in `lines.ts` vocabulary; a value given as "about N units" may be tuned by the agent within §8.6 (3 to 5 for a
notable, one unit for a small, §8.1). Names for new notables are the agent's own plain words, checked by
`names.test.ts`, `ip.test.ts` and a grep of `reference/tree/ref-tree.json` (§2.4).

## 5. Decisions for the user

**U1. Lines the fight uses but the character sheet never counts.** About twenty lines: damage against a bleeding,
poisoned, shocked or low-life target; damage and crit on full life; damage on low life; attack speed after a kill; life
and mana on kill; fervour, insight, grit and warcry durations; knockback; culling strike. Sixteen smalls are worth
nothing on the sheet (Unbloodied's three, Finishing Cut's one, Pain Conduit's spur two, Killing Pace's one, Hot Blood's
three, Flashes of Insight's three, War Drums' three): a player taps one, takes it, and the sheet does not move.
Either (a) the sheet assumes the condition (an engine change: the target is bleeding when the build bleeds, a kill
happened recently, charges are held), or (b) those sixteen smalls are re-lined to the countable half of their notable
(Unbloodied's to life or melee damage, Hot Blood's to attack speed, Flashes of Insight's to spell crit, War Drums' to
warcry effect, Pain Conduit's to life, Killing Pace's and Finishing Cut's to attack speed and bleed damage).
**Recommendation:** (b) now for the smalls, and (a) later for the notables, which keep these lines beside a counted
one. The allocator already weighs several of them, so the measure is not the problem; the sheet is.

**U2. A weapon finished by 120 points, paid for with life.** The strength sword build holds every sword cluster at 120
(sword +4% past it) and gives up life (+82% past it); the sceptre and two-handed fire builds hold the strength side's
fire attacks and their last 40 points buy only life. **Recommendation:** accept it. That is the trade the user's first
example asked for (all the weapon, not all the life), and a third or fourth fire-attack cluster added only to lift a
curve would be supply for the number's sake. If a late direction is wanted anyway, it goes in the Zealot's far west
(Wake, Brazier, Buttress, Thunder Peal) with a partner the build must weigh against life.

**U3. The elemental archer's far clusters.** Its identity stat is flat at 120 because its second and third elemental
clusters (Bright Shafts, Live Wire) are 23 steps out on a road nothing else wants; an inner shortcut alone changed no
plan. Swap Bright Shafts into Killing Eye's place on the Quiver (12 steps, as the other side of the bleed bow's wheel),
with Killing Eye going far? **Recommendation:** yes. It turns a direction nobody sees into a trade (one elemental
cluster near, one far), and Killing Eye is in no plan at any budget today. The elemental bow will get stronger in its
own stat; that is a stat it was cut off from, not one it already had.

**U4. Footwork.** The Reaver's evasion-and-accuracy notable sits 7 steps from the Strider start and is in five builds'
60-point plans (the three bows, dagger-crit, claw-poison). **Recommendation:** no move now. Re-measure after this round's
evasion re-partners (Wanderer, Weaving, Fleet Foot) and decide at audit round 2; if it is still every dexterity
build's first stop, re-partner it then.
