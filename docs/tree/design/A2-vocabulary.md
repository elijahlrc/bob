# A2. The engine's vocabulary

Counted 2026-10-19 from `src/mods/types.ts`, `src/data/statText.ts` (the stat catalogue), the live tree (`getTree()`: baked tree plus
`treeEdits.json`), `src/calc/*` (the sheet), `src/sim/*` (the fight) and `docs/tree/pob-tree.json` (the 3.9 reference tree). Counting and
classification only; nothing here is a design decision. Scratch scripts: `scratchpad/a2/{treeStats,statReads,conds,pobPhrases}.ts`.

## 0. The grammar of a node

A node is a list of `Mod`s. One mod is `stat × kind × tags? × damageTypes? × condition? × per?`:

| Part          | Values                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Rule (`ModDB.matches`)                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kind`        | `base`, `inc`, `more`, `flag`, `override`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `base · (1 + Σinc) · Π more`; the last override wins                                                                                                     |
| `tags`        | 48 skill tags: attack, spell, melee, projectile, area, strike, chaining, dot, ignite, bleed, poison, aura, fire, cold, lightning, the 9 weapon kinds, twoHand, oneHand, dualWield, shield, unarmed, hit, triggered, totem, trap, mine, brand, minion, channelling, duration, curse, warcry, herald, guard, movement, nova, slam, physical, chaos, attackSkill | every tag of the mod must be on the use; `dualWield` and `shield` are put on every use by the character; `attackSkill` reaches ailment damage of attacks |
| `damageTypes` | physical, lightning, cold, fire, chaos                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | must overlap the damage chunk's ancestry (conversion keeps ancestry)                                                                                     |
| `condition`   | 98 ids (`CONDITIONS`), each may be negated                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | sim: evaluated live each decision (`playerConds`, `src/sim/combat.ts`); sheet: a configuration plus `steadyMask`                                         |
| `per`         | `{stat, div}`: value × floor(statValue / div); `statValue` answers str, dex, int, level, charges.grit/fervour/insight, shieldDef, shieldEs                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | nothing else can be a `per` source without a new branch in `Character.statValue`                                                                         |

Stat ids are plain strings; the catalogue with player text is `STAT_TEXT` (about 280 families). There is no "every stat is read" test:
`statText.test.ts` checks that every stat in the data has text, not that the engine reads it.

**The tree today:** 2,618 nodes (6 start, 694 travel, 1,446 small, 445 notable, 27 keystone), 153 distinct stat ids, 32 of the 48
tags, 26 of the 98 conditions. Travel nodes are attributes only (str 283, dex 233, int 178). The tags the tree never uses: strike,
chaining, ignite, hit, triggered, dualWield, shield, unarmed, duration, warcry, herald, guard, movement, nova, slam, physical (the
plan's 2026-10-19 note already rules out slam, strike, nova, chaining, triggered).

**The reference tree:** 1,625 nodes (1,228 small, 369 notable, 28 keystone), 586 distinct line templates once numbers are masked.

## 1. The stats, by theme

Columns: **tree** = nodes carrying it today (s small, n notable, k keystone, t travel); **sheet** = a `src/calc` file reads it
(`items.ts` alone means a local item stat, not a character stat); **sim** = obeyed in a fight. The rule for **sim**: the fight never
reads most stats by name; it consumes the sheet's `profile()` and `defence()` fields (`p.lifeOnHit`, `profile.pierce`, `radiusMult`...),
so a stat the sheet folds into one of those is obeyed, and `sheetVsSim.*.test.ts` holds the two together. "direct" = the sim reads
the stat itself. Families (`chargeOn.*`) are one row.

## 2. Conditions: what sets them, and whether they mean anything here

`LOW_LIFE = 0.35`, `RECENT = 4` s, `OVERLOAD_TIME = 8` s (`src/data/constants.ts`, `src/sim/combat.ts`). The sheet assumes a
configuration: `steadyMask` turns on hitRecently, usedFlaskRecently, every buff the build can grant, targetCursed when it hexes,
killedRecently when clearing, onLowLife when 65% of life is reserved, critRecently at 20% crit; the sim has what is true.

## 4. What the reference tree says that Bob cannot say

From the 586 line templates of `pob-tree.json` (counts are lines on the 3.9 tree). Four buckets. Judged against `STAT_TEXT` and the
engine, not against the tree: most of the task's examples turn out to be stats already.

> Correction (2026-10-19, `D-engine-stats.md`): bucket A's "warcry cooldown recovery" is **not** live (utility cooldowns are stored
> raw and never scaled); `skillDuration` tagged `mine` and `aoe` tagged `minion` are dead too (a mine's life and a minion's splash
> ignore them). Three bucket-B stats were investigated there: only `warcryEffect` is worth adding.

## 5. What a node can say about items and about attributes

**Items.** The character carries a `weaponTags` set built from its hands (`weaponStats(main)`, a second hand when dual wielding, or an
`unarmed` hand with `FISTS` when nothing is held): the nine weapon kinds plus `oneHand`/`twoHand`, and on every skill use `dualWield`
when both hands hold weapons and `shield` when the off-hand's base class is `shield`. So a node may key on a weapon kind as a tag
("with Swords", on the use) or as a static condition (`wieldingSword`, true for the fight: 437 tree nodes do this), on `dualWielding`,
`holdingShield`, `unarmed`, and may scale from the shield's defences (`per: shieldDef`/`shieldEs`, `shieldDefences`, `shieldEs`). It
cannot speak of armour slots, item rarity, sockets, quivers or a weapon's base damage; `local` mods are an item's own. Gear gates on
attributes: `itemReq` (bases × `REQUIREMENT_SCALE`, cut by `reqReduction` on the item) is checked in `src/run/inventory.ts` before a
swap, and a gem's natural level is capped by `gemAttrReq` (full = 10 to 98 by level 20; hybrids 60% each, `calc/gems.ts`).

**Attributes.** Per point (`src/data/constants.ts`, DESIGN 6.2): strength +0.5 life and 0.2% increased melee physical damage (and
projectile attacks under Strongarm, spells under Iron Will, physical projectiles under Iron Grip); dexterity +2 accuracy and 0.2%
increased evasion (none under Plated Hide); intelligence +0.5 mana and 0.2% increased energy shield. `allAttr` adds to all three and
`inc` on an attribute multiplies it (`attr = round((base + all) × mult)`). A node may use any attribute as a `per` source
(`per: {stat: 'str', div: 10}`), which the tree does not do today. The six classes start with their own attribute mix (DESIGN 5.2);
travel nodes give +10 of one and nothing else, so attributes are the tree's only currency for gear and gem requirements.
